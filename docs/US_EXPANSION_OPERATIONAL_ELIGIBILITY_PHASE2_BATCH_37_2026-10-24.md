## Priority airport-specific findings

### MYF — Montgomery–Gibbs Executive, California: direction-specific runway length

[City of San Diego](https://www.sandiego.gov/airports/montgomery) identifies main RW10L/28R **4,598 × 150 feet**, but **RW28R has a 1,176-foot displaced threshold**, giving approximately **3,422 feet** of physical approach-side landing pavement after the threshold. Secondary 10R/28L is **3,401 × 60 feet** and 5/23 is **3,400 × 75 feet**, each below Airlinerra's 1,200m paved-runway screen. The [FAA airport safety briefing](https://www.faa.gov/flight_deck/myf) additionally describes closely spaced parallel runways, wrong-surface landing risk and complicated San Diego controlled airspace. Some legitimate GA, charter, medical and light cargo activity exists, but aircraft suitability/declared LDA **must be assessed by approach direction**; no blanket automatic eligibility or closure.

### O22 — Columbia, California: takeoff-only restriction with an end date

[October 2026 FAA-derived NOTAM](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/O22) states **RW11 closed to takeoff until December 5, 2026**. This specific directional limitation does not mean the whole airport is shut; alternate runway directions and any other active notices require verification. Tuolumne County lists general aviation, charter businesses and firefighting operations. Validate actual operator licenses and the intended aircraft/route.

### OJC — Johnson County Executive, Kansas: positive charter evidence

[Johnson County's official airport page](https://www.jocogov.org/department/airport-commission/executive-airport) confirms **three full-service FBOs** and on-field charter businesses supporting small aircraft through corporate jets on an approximately **4,097-foot** runway. This is affirmative on-demand commercial-aircraft evidence, not proof of a named current Part135 certificate for a given flight or a scheduled airline. Validate operator and airframe/owner permission separately.

### MAO — Marion County, South Carolina: owner lists actual holiday airport closures

[South Carolina Aeronautics airport information](https://aeronautics.sc.gov/airports/marion-county-airport) states the airport **closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day**, unlike ordinary 07–17 staffing or after-hours fuel by phone. Check exact actual holiday/date NOTAM and owner exceptions before interpreting airport availability. Jet A is listed but mechanical repairs are not.

### MAC — Macon Downtown, Georgia: observed lack of 2024 commercial activity, not timeless absence

[TBI Airport Management's MAC page](https://www.tbiam.aero/mac) reports **no commercial activity in 2024** and identifies Macon Downtown as general aviation serving business aircraft. This is period-specific historical evidence, **not** proof no 2026 Part135 flights or paid charters can occur.

### MBO — Bruce Campbell Field, Mississippi: charter flights are documented

[City of Madison](https://madisonthecity.com/government/airport/) identifies corporate and **charter** users and Madison Air aviation FBO services. This is positive on-demand aviation evidence; the actual licensed carrier, fleet and any scheduled routes still need separate verification.

### MYZ — Marysville Municipal, Kansas: owner says no fuel and 12,500lb weight limit

[City of Marysville](https://www.cityofmarysvilleks.com/airport) explicitly states **NO FUEL** and a **12,500-pound airport aircraft weight limit**. Those are material dispatch and pavement/airplane suitability concerns despite publicly available paved runway; confirmation of the exact gear rating and airport permission is needed.

### MQI — Dare County, North Carolina: only primary runway qualifies by nominal length

[Dare County airport authority](https://www.darenc.gov/departments/airport) lists primary **4,305 × 100ft** paved and separate **3,300 × 75ft** crosswind runway, below the 1,200m paved screen. Seasonal tower and Outer Banks coastal weather are actual operational considerations. The shorter runway must not inherit primary capacity.

### ONZ — Grosse Ile, Michigan: fishing charters are not air charter

[Grosse Ile airport's pilot services](https://www.grosseileairport.com/pilot-information) lists aviation fuel, flight training and helicopter services, but references to fishing charter businesses are **maritime**, not evidence of an FAA-certified aviation charter. Preserve clear source/entity identity and verify any actual air carrier separately.

### OIC — Norwich, New York: no FBO versus self-service fuel

[Chenango County airport](https://www.chenangocountyny.gov/285/Airport-Fee-Schedule) reports **no current full-service FBO**, but self-service avgas/Jet A is listed. Limited staffed handling is not equivalent to permanent runway closure.
