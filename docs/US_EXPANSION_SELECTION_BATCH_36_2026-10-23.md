## Commercial-eligibility policy

An airport operator service page, state planning role or FAA registration is **not** legal or operational authority for scheduled flights, a particular Part 121/135 carrier, transport-category aircraft or commercial cargo. Recheck current NOTAMs and airport operator terms where there are restrictions; separately assess aircraft takeoff/landing and weight limitations, current airline/charter/cargo service, and Part 139 certification *where applicable*. Do not automatically activate or approve any airport.

Exactly six Airlinerra categories remain: **Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub**. Do not translate a US airport system planning label or FAA Part 139 class directly to an Airlinerra tier.
