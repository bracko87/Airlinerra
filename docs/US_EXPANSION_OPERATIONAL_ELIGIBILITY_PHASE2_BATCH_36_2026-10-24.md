The existing candidate 1,200-metre operational paved runway threshold is only the first gate: usable direction-specific landing distance, wheel and width capacity, effective FAA notices, terrain/weather, current airport-owner permission, legally certificated airline/charter/cargo operators and ground services must also be screened. An operating constraint hold is **not necessarily an airport-wide permanent closure**; an evidence gap does not mean all paid flights are unlawful or absent.

## Priority findings

### L45 — Bakersfield Municipal, California: dated runway closures and separate airport from BFL

[Current L45 NOTAM listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/L45) identifies closure of RW16/34 **October 19–24 through 14:00 UTC**, plus a separate closure **October 25–31 daily 01:30–14:00 UTC**, and a nearby October 24–25 UAS notice. None is a permanent runway closure. The airport is **Bakersfield Municipal L45**, not scheduled-airline **Meadows Field BFL**; the candidate registry name 'Bakersfield International Airport' requires care to avoid cross-airport service attribution. Match actual closure and local flight times before any proposed route.

### K62 — Gene Snyder Airport, Kentucky: runway load and constrained visual approach aid

[FAA-derived current airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/K62) gives **4,002 × 75ft** asphalt runway with **12,500lb single-wheel** published weight rating, PAPI unusable more than **8 degrees left or 6 degrees right** of runway centerline, and trees near RW21. Runway LDA may be physically 4,002ft in each direction but aircraft gear load and approach obstruction suitability remain separate checks. Airfield access is not proof of carrier certification.

### LZZ — Lampasas Municipal, Texas: future runway extension is not present pavement

[City airport description](https://www.lampasas.org/117/Airport) identifies an existing approximately **4,200ft** paved strip, emergency medical helipad, 24-hour fuel and **proposed** extension to **5,000ft**. The extension must **not** be used as an existing runway in eligibility, and medical flights are not an unrestricted public airline or airfreight operating certificate.

### LCG — Wayne Municipal, Nebraska: runways are not interchangeable

[Wayne airport authority page](https://cityofwayne.org/75/Airport) reports main paved **RW17/35 4,200 × 75ft**, separate paved **RW04/22 3,400 × 60ft**, and **1,674ft turf strip**. Only the long main currently clears the 1,200m paved-runway screen by nominal length; the others cannot inherit it. Each individual runway needs current LDA/weight/obstacle and operating checks.

### L77 — Chiriaco Summit, California: narrow, remote desert airfield

[FAA airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/L77) establishes a roughly **4,000 × 50ft** paved public strip with no assured on-field fuel/repairs and remote high-desert service. A county lease/owner-use check and airplane width, temperature and terrain capacity are essential before paid charters.

### LKP, LLJ and L22: terrain and aircraft performance

**LKP Lake Placid, New York** is a highland Adirondack airfield requiring current mountain/weather/winter departure and landing checks. **LLJ Challis, Idaho** has a 4,600 × 60ft-class paved mountain airfield with visual approaches; the Idaho planning Local label does not mean in-game Local approval. **L22 Yucca Valley, California** has a 60ft-class paved desert runway requiring heat/density-altitude and gear-width assessment. None is blanket permanently excluded by terrain alone.

### LPC — Lompoc, California: skydiving and commercial marketing

[Lompoc city airport](https://www.cityoflompoc.com/government/departments/public-works/transportation/lompoc-airport) publishes a **4,600 × 100ft** field, fuel and skydiving activity. Its commercial-oriented airport mission is not a real current Part135 operating certificate or airline schedule; check drop-zone/NOTAM and aircraft/operator rights.

### LHB — Hearne, Texas: glider operations and old service evidence

[Historic Texas DOT airport directory](https://ftp.txdot.gov/pub/txdot-info/d/airport_directory/lhb.pdf) describes 4,001 × 75ft runway, avgas, no repairs and glider use. The old directory does not prove current fuel, commercial support or certified air carrier and glider coordination remains distinct.

### M12 — Steele, Missouri: very limited length margin

[FAA airport listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/M12) gives approximately **3,984 × 60ft** runway (about **1,214m**). Nominal length only slightly exceeds 1,200m; threshold displacements, obstacles or aircraft loading can invalidate a proposed aircraft landing operation even while the runway is open.

### M17 — Bolivar Municipal, Missouri: paved and turf rules differ

[October 2026 NOTAM listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/M17) includes RW36 PAPI out **October 14–31** and separate constraints on turf RW17/35. Neither is proof the paved RW18/36 is permanently closed. Check live FAA notice by date and actual aircraft/approach.
