- **Steamboat Springs Bob Adams SBS:** FAA-derived published declared **LDA 3852ft (~1174m)** both RW14 and RW32, despite full physical **4452ft (~1357m)**. RW32 displaced threshold about 600ft and mountain/high-altitude restrictions add further limits. Since LDA is below 1200m, SBS remains **held**, not admitted. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/SBS
- **North Central State SFZ:** RI operator and FAA source distinguish paved **05/23 5000x100ft S-30/D-60** from **15/33 3211x75ft S-12.5**, below Airlinerra basic paved length threshold. The smaller crosswind runway does not make the qualifying primary physically closed. Winter, noise, parachutes, gliders, airport owner and operator approval are still pending. - **Gillespie Field SEE:** County-owned public training/GA operations and Class D tower traffic do not permanently close the runway. However, flight-school traffic is not proof of certified charter service and ATC/airport permission, dimensions and weight require independent checks. Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/dpw/airports/gillespie.html
- **Stuttgart SGT and Stevens Point STE:** Crop dusting, medical flights, law enforcement and flight training require flight-specific separation. Their presence alone does not mean a permanent physical runway closure or prove other commercial air-service rights.
- **Sonora SOA:** Physical runway ~4037ft, displaced threshold **RW36 ~601ft and RW18 ~93ft**. Counting entire paved distance would overlook landing directions that can be under 1200m usable distance. Bad markings and obstacles remain unresolved.
- **Spencer SPW:** Multiple concrete runways are distinct; RW30 displacement and 2026 REIL outages on another runway need current dated NOTAM checks.
- **Craig Field SEM:** Physical mixed asphalt/concrete ~8000ft but FAA source notes POOR pavement. Length and tower cannot override runway condition.
- **George M Bryan STF:** County statement of no commercial flights cannot be treated as proof of charter/cargo privileges. Written clarification is required.
- **All admissions:** Airlinerra uses exactly **six pre-agreed airport categories**, never four. None is assigned here. Game approval requires **operational asphalt/concrete runway with at least 1200m usable direction-specific LDA**, aircraft-suitable width/pavement load and climb/weather, live NOTAM, fuel/emergency/handling support, written commercial owner permission and a genuinely certificated passenger/cargo or charter operator.
