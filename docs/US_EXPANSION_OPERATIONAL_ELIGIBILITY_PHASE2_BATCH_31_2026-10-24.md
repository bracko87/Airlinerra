Even though it narrowly exceeds 1,200m length, **6m width requires a strict airplane-specific operating check**, not automatic airline fleet approval.

**3TR — Jerry Tyler Memorial, Michigan.** [FAA-derived runway data](https://www.airnav.com/airport/3TR) lists RW15/33 **4,100 × 75ft** with a 300ft displaced RW15 threshold; separate RW04/22 is **3,316 × 55ft, cracked/broken**, with large threshold displacements. The secondary fails minimum length and is in poor condition, but main RW33 can be assessed separately.

**1M9 — Lake Barkley, Kentucky.** The public paved 4,800ft runway has displaced thresholds and approximately 4,600ft usable landing distance both directions. Owner permission and actual Part135 operator remain unverified.

**42M — Thayer Memorial, Missouri.** FAA chart supplement documents a **4,200 × 50ft** runway, **1.1% slope**, nonstandard lighting, trees and safety-area issues requiring aircraft-specific investigation.

**20U — Beach, North Dakota.** 4,200 × 60ft runway, **12,500lb single-wheel load** and snow clearing by arrangement; fuel exists but maintenance remains unverified.

**33N — Delaware Airpark.** Owner publishes **no full-service FBO** and 24-hour **100LL self-service**; do not assume turbine fuel or unrestricted commercial carrier operations.

**46D — Carrington, North Dakota.** City advertises year-round charter landings; a real positive lead, not an identified current Part135 certificate or scheduled airline.

**4B6 — Ticonderoga, New York.** Owner says airport is open; winter snow can cause date-specific NOTAM closures, not a blanket permanent closure.
