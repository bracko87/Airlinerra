## Interpretation and next verification requirements

- Source agreement is principally between FAA-derived **secondary** web directories and Phase 4 documentation. APF and VDF also have published Florida DOT inspection evidence. Do **not** claim this is a flight-date official FAA/NOTAM validation or a legal operating permission.
- In particular, **PAAQ** has secondary-source disagreement over RW16 LDA; **MDD** and **TTD** have a listed LDA for one direction but not the opposite direction in the cited extracted record; **ARR** likewise requires the other end of 09/27 to be independently established.
- **BAK**, **MVN** and **OSU** have published restrictions on certain scheduled or large nonscheduled air-carrier passenger operations. Apply constraints to the specific service type; do not infer that a runway is wholly closed or that a different carrier category is automatically permitted.
- **SUA** has EMAS outside the usable runway pavement and multiple independently rated runways. Do not add EMAS or subtract displaced threshold twice from a published LDA.
- All 26 need official per-direction FAA revalidation; selected airplane/gear/obstacle performance; owner written commercial rights; actual operator certificate; date-valid NOTAM/FICON/weather; and fuel/handling/rescue suitability before any gameplay decision.
- All 26 remain `NOT_APPROVED`; no final six-category assignments or world activations.
