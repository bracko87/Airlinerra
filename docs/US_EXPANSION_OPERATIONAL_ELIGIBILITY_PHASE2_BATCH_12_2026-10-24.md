## High-impact findings and interpretations

### TKI / DTX — McKinney National, Texas: airport certified, airline service is FUTURE

The [FAA announced a new Part 139 Airport Operating Certificate on September 24, 2026](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/faa-grants-commercial-operating-certificate-mckinney-national-airport), and the [city's October 23 report](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/m/NewsFlash/Home/Detail/7047) confirmed the aircraft rescue/firefighting and airfield safety requirements. Avelo's [published service schedule](https://www.mckinneytexas.org/m/newsflash/home/detail/6915) says **first flights begin November 11, 2026**, followed by additional routes in December. As of this report's **October 24** review date, they are **planned, not yet operating**. **FAA identifier TKI and IATA passenger code DTX refer to the same airport**, not separate airfields. The correct status is positive *future* commercial evidence with game admission still pending.

### MKC — Charles B. Wheeler Downtown, Missouri: specific seat-count operating rule

[Official airport charter/services page](https://flymkc.com/services) states **commercial air carriers with more than 30 passenger seats require prior permission from the Airport Authority**; ARFF coordination also applies. This is a specific restriction for qualifying flights, **not** a prohibition on all charter or cargo aircraft. Owner confirmation, airline certification and aircraft seat count must be obtained before a new Airlinerra route.

### YIP — Willow Run, Michigan: actual substantial cargo operations

[Willow Run airport's official cargo page](https://www.willowrunairport.com/cargo) documents cargo operations with **Kalitta Air, Kalitta Charters, Ameristar and USA Jet** named as cargo carriers and very substantial freight volume. This is **positive current cargo-operating evidence**, not an airport without commercial activity. The Phase 2 `EVIDENCE_GAP_HOLD` means **specific aircraft, operator certificates and game admission** have not been completed, not that cargo is absent.

### VIS — Visalia, California: Essential Air Service still prospective

[City Council airport EAS document](https://visalia.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=25B115EF-A567-4A6B-88C6-0060D4DD20BB&ID=8217953) explains re-eligibility begins **May 1, 2027**, with 2026 local carrier-review preparations. Do not count a 2027 eligibility date as existing 2026 scheduled passenger flights.

### BKL — Burke Lakefront, Ohio: redevelopment proposal is not an airport closure

The city proposed airport closure/redevelopment, but an [August 17, 2026 report](https://www.axios.com/local/cleveland/2026/08/17/faa-burke-airport-closure-plan-redevelopment-lake-erie) documents FAA opposition. The airport cannot honestly be marked *currently closed* purely due to the proposal. Future operating viability, runway/NOTAM condition and owner permissions remain to be checked.

### Additional operational constraints

- **TYL Taylor Airport, AZ** has a published **12,500lb single-wheel** pavement limitation despite a nominal 7,200ft strip, so do not allocate heavy aircraft solely from length. [Town](https://www.tayloraz.gov/airport/).
- **SUS Spirit of St Louis, MO** lists business/charter and FAA Class IV status, but its FAQ cautions heavy, sustained jet operations exceed some runway structural limits. Verify type-specific pavement loading and certificate scope. [Airport operator](https://www.spiritairport.com/).
- **RMG Richard B Russell, GA** reports military C-130 drops on particular weekdays, as well as deer/ultralight traffic. These should be evaluated as special traffic/operating constraints, **not** evidence of commercial cargo. [FAA airport master](https://enasr.faa.gov/eNASR/nasr/Current/Airport/14406).
- **06A Moton Field, AL** has source-reported approach PAPI unavailability for both runway directions and wildlife warnings; confirm whether repaired using up-to-date NOTAMs. [FAA-derived airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/06A).
- **OLU Columbus, NE** has separate concrete main and turf crosswind runways; the turf strip does not satisfy the game paved-runway requirement. [Municipal operator](https://www.columbusne.us/280/Airport-Information).
- **DUB (legacy U25), HDL (legacy 0J6) and IYA (legacy 0R3)** require historical FAA code reconciliation; do not create duplicates or overwrite immutable keys simply to harmonize display codes.

## Nonadmission and integrity safeguards

1. **50 new rows with 50 distinct immutable keys**; each has evidence URL(s), finding, specific next action, effective date and timestamp. The record was updated only from `PENDING`.
2. **All 1,929 expansion candidates remain `NOT_APPROVED` for gameplay**. No `airport_world_activation` records were added; game profile categories were not approved; initial roster stayed at 3,140.
