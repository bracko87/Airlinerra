## Important research distinctions and safeguards

- **Angelina County (LFK):** County operator confirms **two asphalt runways: 07/25 5398x100ft and 16/34 4309x100ft**; the FAA-source condition and aircraft load classes differ (**S-60/D-90 primary** versus **S-30 secondary**). Airport operator page and October FAA-derived review agree on the separate-runway principle, although some owner/legacy materials contain runway-number variations. Do not disqualify the longer qualifying paved main because the secondary has lesser limits, and do not assign primary load ratings to the secondary. FAA RW34 also reports ~107ft displaced threshold. Sources: https://www.angelinacounty.gov/airport/ and https://www.airnav.com/airport/LFK
- **Los Alamos (LAM):** Los Alamos County confirms research/business GA, Classic Air medical transport and aerial firefighting; the current airport page reports **self-service Jet A/100LL with no fuel truck and no on-field mechanic services**. These users are traffic-coordination obligations, not a permanent closure of its otherwise operational paved runway. The advance does not verify commercial carrier privileges or high-terrain performance. Source: https://www.losalamosnm.gov/Services/Los-Alamos-Airport
- **Leesburg Executive (JYO):** Washington DC **Special Flight Rules Area** and **24-hour prior customs reservation** are substantial regulatory/administrative gates, not a generic airport admission. Remains held; no flights may be assumed authorized without actual SFRA and CBP clearance.
- **Big Bear City (L35):** Owner states on-field charter services are **not available** in the recorded current research; existing medevac/scenic helicopters are not a substitute for a certifiable scheduled commercial airplane operator. The roughly 6752ft mountain elevation also requires exceptional airplane performance calculations.
- **Jetmore (K79):** Paved asphalt/concrete runway is reported **POOR**; no amount of nominal length authorizes use by a commercial airplane without structural inspection and strength rating.
- **Abilene (K78), Kansas versus Hillsboro (INJ), Texas:** Preserve identities separate from other US airports named Hillsboro or Abilene; verify 2026 official runway designation because some city and FAA sources disagree. Hillsboro nominal length is barely more than 1200m and cannot imply qualifying declared LDA.
- **Campbell County/Colonel Stiner (JAU):** Older airport directory warns of RW23 downhill slope, and FBO hours are not year-round 24h fuel coverage. Check current FAA slope and usable distance before aircraft allocation.
- **Trona (L72) and California City (L71):** High desert airframe performance, width and fuel/handling constraints demand aircraft-by-aircraft confirmation rather than general GA access.
- **Hearne (LHB):** Historical glider and 100LL listings alone cannot prove 2026 separation procedures, fuel or licensed airline activity; remains held.
- **MidCoast/Wright Army Airfield (LHW):** FAA joint-use military designation is not unrestricted civil-airline access. Army and civilian operators must separately authorize intended use.
- **Airlinerra's established exactly six airport categories** remain the only allowed taxonomy and none have been assigned in this Phase 3 batch. Game admission still requires an **operational asphalt/concrete runway of 1200m or more direction-specific usable LDA**, suitable aircraft loads/width and terrain/weather, current FAA NOTAM, fuel/emergency services, local airport permission and applicable certified carrier/operator authority.
