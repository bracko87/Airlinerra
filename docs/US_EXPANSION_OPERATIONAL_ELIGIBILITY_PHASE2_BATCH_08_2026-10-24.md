## High-priority findings

**O61 Cameron Park (CA)** — 4,051ft physical paved runway, but displaced RW13/RW31 thresholds **193ft / 1,509ft**, leaving estimated landing spans **3,858ft (~1,176m)** and **2,542ft (~775m)** respectively. The airport's own documentation cautions about the substantial RW31 displacement; published FAA notes limit use to aircraft **less than 12,500lb max certificated weight and less than 50ft wingspan**. **Provisional ineligible only under a 1,200m *usable landing distance* rule**, pending formal resolution whether Airlinerra's agreed 1,200m minimum means pavement length or declared LDA. The nominal paved physical length itself is above 1,200m; do not misclassify that as a short physical runway. [Owner's runway guide](https://www.cameronparkairport.org/flight-planning) · [FAA-derived runway and restrictions](https://www.airnav.com/airport/O61).

**RGK Red Wing Regional** — physically **in Wisconsin**, although owned and operated by the City of Red Wing, Minnesota and FAA describes proximity to Red Wing. Candidate `faa_state=MN` is therefore not an accurate *physical state* if consumed as geolocation. Retain original FAA administrative metadata and key while separately reconciling geographical attributes and game country/state location. Owner lists runway **5,010×100ft**, **30,000lb single wheel / 75,000lb dual wheel**, and commercial operator minimum standards. [City of Red Wing](https://www.redwingmn.gov/363/About-the-Airport).

**P13 San Carlos Apache (AZ)** — tribal airport NOTAMs as of 24 Oct include **100LL unavailable through 31 Oct**, RW09 and RW27 lighting/PAPI outages extending into spring 2027, and AWOS service notice. Tribal permission and live NOTAM checks must precede any approval. [Published FAA-derived NOTAMs](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/P13).

**OXR Oxnard (CA)** — operational public-use airport, **not physically closed** merely because historical Part139 listing shows inactive; FAA-derived remarks require unscheduled commercial aircraft with **more than nine passenger seats** to coordinate with the airport and confirm available ARFF. No assumed blanket commercial approval. [FAA-derived operating remarks](https://skyvector.com/airport/OXR/Oxnard-Airport).

**PVW Hale County (TX)** — main RW04/22 pavement has reported **longitudinal/transverse cracks up to 4 inches**, while separate RW13/31 paved surface is **EXCELLENT** and about 4,000ft long. Record runway-specific conditions and evaluate wheel loads; do not label entire airport unusable. [FAA-derived runway records](https://www.airnav.com/airport/PVW).

**RCX Rusk County (WI)** — main RW14/32 FAIR around 4,001ft; secondary 01/19 POOR around 3,199ft. Short/poor secondary does not automatically exclude main paved runway. [Airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/RCX).

**MIW, MFI, ONY** — each has multiple runways with different pavement length/quality, so evaluate by direction and surface. **MVI** has high elevation and 12,500lb runway strength; **N24** has owner/access and runway marking constraints; **OTG** has a time-bounded instrument-approach outage. Follow-up actions are preserved per airport.
