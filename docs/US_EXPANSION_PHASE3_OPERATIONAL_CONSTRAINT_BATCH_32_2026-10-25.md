- **Wakefield Municipal AKQ, Virginia:** RW03/21 nominal **4,337x75ft** asphalt with displaced thresholds about **298ft RW03** and **471ft RW21**. RW21 nominal after threshold displacement is only **3,866ft**, below 1,200m; RW03 is about 4,039ft before any further declared landing restrictions. - **Gruver Municipal E19 and Sells E78:** FAA-derived 2026 screening lists **POOR** asphalt on both. A long enough physical runway is not evidence that pavement strength and surface quality are safe for an aircraft; both remain held.
- **Colorado Plains Regional AKO:** Agricultural spray aircraft during spring-to-autumn activity are a local movement coordination and NOTAM matter, **not a categorical permanent airport closure**. This narrow issue advanced, but faded markings and 4,716ft-elevation aircraft performance still require assessment. [FAA-derived source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/AKO).
- **West Bend Municipal ETB:** Military helicopter practice near a military-only helipad is not a permanent paved airplane-runway shutdown. Actual traffic safety/airspace restrictions, usable landing distances, operator and owner commercial rights remain independent checks. [FAA-derived source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/ETB).
- **Heart of Georgia Regional EZM:** Flight-school/tower activity and wildlife alerts are not blanket runway closure. Verify flight-date notices, airframe-compatible LDA/weight and an actually licensed paid service; the airport name does not establish a game category.
- **Alturas AAT, Hatch E05 and Edwards County ECU:** Narrow **50–60ft** pavement, high-altitude or closely adjacent excavation and dirt mounds require aircraft-specific runway width, clearance, obstacles and ground safety verification.
- **Arco/Butte County AOC and Alpine Casparis E38:** Published RNAV/obstacle departure procedures demonstrate procedure availability, **not** proof that the aircraft meets actual mountain/high-desert climb requirements or has commercial landing rights.
- **San Gabriel Valley EMT:** Nominal pavement about **1,218m** has minimal margin over 1,200m; airport reliever status or LA metro traffic does not replace a published direction-specific LDA, loading limit or owner paid-flight permission.
- **Llano AQO, Pike County EOP, Cameron EZZ and Upton County E48:** Self-service avgas, uncertain call-ahead turbine fuel and unattended/irregular staffing do not prove commercial aircraft turnaround, reliable emergency response or currently licensed providers.

**Eligibility rules protected:** Only an operational **asphalt/concrete runway** with an individually verified, direction-specific **usable landing distance at least 1,200m**, appropriate airframe runway width/pavement loading and validated approach/climb/terrain/weather, actual date-valid NOTAM, available fuel and emergency ground service, written owner commercial-operation rights and a properly certificated carrier can ever be considered for game admission.
