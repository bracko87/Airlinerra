## Review methodology and safeguards

The initial, separate Phase 1 FAA/airport-specific source research was already completed across **1,929** US expansion airports. Phase 2 reexamines runway-specific constraints, private-aircraft and charter evidence, current airline schedules, airport operator rights, dated changes and identity/alias discrepancies. Each row has source URLs, original reference dates, a particular source-based finding and follow-up action plus timestamp. All findings were independently read back from `airlinerra.airport_operational_triage`; first-pass research remains available in `airlinerra.airport_commercial_verification`.

This is *eligibility evidence triage*, not flight clearance, air-carrier certification, an airport manager's authorization, a final rejection, or Airlinerra gameplay admission. FAA operating notices and FBO services change over time. Source-reported airport-wide closures must be separated from short turf-runway winter closure, tower hours, fuel unavailability or event-specific landing permission; airport names and government planning tiers are not the game's six airport categories.

## Priority Batch 20 findings

### HEZ — Natchez–Adams County Airport, Mississippi: REAL scheduled airline service

[Local airport development authority's July 1, 2026 launch announcement](https://natchezinc.com/natchez-adams-county-airport-hez-welcomes-united-airlines-to-new-commercial-terminal/) confirms **United Express service operated by SkyWest** connecting **HEZ ↔ Houston George Bush Intercontinental (IAH)** and a newly converted passenger terminal. [Cooperative Energy's September 14, 2026 article](https://cooperativeenergy.com/cooperative-energys-2026-spark-awarded-to-the-natchez-adams-county-airport-commercial-terminal-project/) confirms the actual daily flight launch, not merely an announced proposal. [October 24 dated timetable](https://2lnr.com/routes/hez-iah) lists **United UA5139**, a **CRJ200**, on October 24 and subsequent dates. This is **affirmative operating scheduled airline evidence**; it must not be classified as a GA field lacking airline service. It is nevertheless **not automatically approved** as a new Airlinerra airport or for all aircraft/flight types: runway load, separate legal fleet/airport constraints, and game admission still require review.

### HND — Henderson Executive, Nevada: event PPR windows, don't carry expired October restrictions forward

[Henderson Executive's airport operator](https://www.hnd.aero/) publishes prior-permission-required events:
- NBAA-BACE **October 16–23, 2026**;
- Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix **November 17–23, 2026**;
- National Finals Rodeo **December 2–13, 2026**;
- College championship **January 20–26, 2027**.

As of **October 24, 2026**, the October 16–23 event PPR period is **past**, so it must not be presented as a still-effective blanket flight prohibition; the **future** periods are relevant to later game days. The same airport [business tenant directory](https://hnd.aero/Business) lists charter and fractional providers; actual certificates/aircraft require their own verification. The operator publishes a 24-hour airfield but limited tower/FBO staffed hours: avoid interpreting staffing as a runway closure.

### OZS — Camdenton Memorial, Missouri: former FAA code H21 versus current OZS

The 1,929-candidate registry stores the historic `source_ident=KH21`, while contemporary FAA-derived [OZS airport listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/OZS) uses `OZS/KOZS`. `SOURCE_IDENTITY_RECONCILIATION` is an **alias/source lineage task**, not permission to create a duplicate airport, rewrite the immutable `OURAIRPORTS:` key or assign a game tier. The airport continues to require normal carrier and runway authorization checks.

### GTU — Georgetown Executive, Texas: aircraft loading limit

[FAA-derived Georgetown Executive airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/GTU) lists a paved **approximately 5,004ft** runway with **30,000lb single-wheel** and **60,000lb dual-wheel** ratings. These are different aircraft landing-gear loading limits, not scheduled-route permissions. Match each selected aircraft's gross/gear rating and declared landing distance before any fleet candidate is considered.

### GWR and HYR — short turf secondary runway ≠ closed paved primary

**Gwinner/Roger Melroe Field, ND:** [FAA Chart Supplement transcription](https://tallyaero.com/atlas/chart-supplement/NC_339/) documents a **5,000×75ft paved primary** and separate turf runway, with seasonal winter turf closure, wildlife warnings, unattended operations and 24-hour self-service Jet A/100LL. Do not classify the entire airport closed by the *turf* runway's winter closure.

**Sawyer County, WI:** [FAA-derived airport/FBO listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KHYR) notes about **5,003×100ft paved primary** and a **1,088ft secondary turf** runway; only the paved runway can qualify under the 1,200m asphalt/concrete rule. Winter operation and aircraft loading still require verification.

### HDO — four runways at South Texas Regional, Texas

[Hondo city airport page](https://www.hondo-tx.org/161/South-Texas-Regional-Airport) reports **four distinct active runways**. They must be checked individually for pavement, length, declared landing distances, load and approach; applying the longest runway to every departure or commercial aircraft is unsound. A published FBO is not evidence of an unrestricted airline license.

- **GSH Goshen, Indiana:** [Sweet Aviation's locations page](https://www.sweetaviation.com/locations/) specifically identifies Goshen Municipal base and advertised charters, as does the city [airport website](https://flygoshen.com/). Positive charter services; carrier certificates/aircraft unverified.
- **GVL Gainesville, Georgia:** published Lanier Flight Express charter aviation business; verify current charter credentials rather than claiming absence of paid flights.
- **HUM Houma-Terrebonne, Louisiana:** [Airport authority's aviation tenant page](https://houma-airport.com/airport-aviation/) lists offshore helicopter operators Bristow, PHI and Westwind plus general-charter businesses. Helicopter crew transport is **not** automatically scheduled fixed-wing airline service and requires aircraft/runway-specific eligibility.
- **HWD Hayward Executive, California:** [City of Hayward](https://www.hayward-ca.gov/your-government/departments/hayward-executive-airport) identifies charter, cargo/humanitarian and medevac activity, but tower hours and noise procedures constrain scheduling. This is positive real commercial aviation evidence, not automatic acceptance of every aircraft.
