## Priority technical and commercial-service findings

### PTK — Oakland County International, Michigan

[Oakland County's airport business directory](https://www.oaklandcountymi.gov/community/airports/airport-businesses) lists several actual charter businesses (**Corporate Flight, DCT Aviation, IFL Group, Pentastar, Royal Air, Premier Private Jets**) and freight companies (**IFL Group, Royal Air**). The [county airport page](https://www.oaklandcountymi.gov/community/airports/oakland-county-international-airport) also documents **2026 Taxiway D reconstruction**. These are affirmative commercial-service leads with possible time-specific taxiway and ground-operating constraints. Confirm current Part 135/cargo operator certificates, fleet and individual work-phase notices before admission. Do not mark commercial charter absent or assume the field is closed.

### POU — Dutchess County, New York

The [county charter services page](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/Airport/Charter-Services.htm) lists **Fly Personal**. Historic scheduled airline activity ended around 2001 according to earlier transportation planning research. A county-charter directory link is positive lead, **not** independent proof that a particular Part 135 carrier certificate or regularly scheduled airline exists.

### PRB, RDG, SPA and STP — on-demand service is not scheduled airline service

- [Paso Robles city](https://www.prcity.com/352/Airport-Overview) states no scheduled airline service but confirms charter, ambulance and firefighting operations.
- [Reading Regional authority](https://readingairport.org/faqs) cites Part 135/group charter and cargo handling with no scheduled passenger airline. An Orlando group flight is charter evidence, not an unrestricted scheduled route.
- [Spartanburg Downtown city](https://www.spartanburgdowntownairport.com/present) lists charter offerings and forklift cargo loading. A cargo forklift does not prove a cargo airline.
- [Saint Paul Downtown airport authority](https://metroairports.org/our-airports/st-paul-downtown-airport) lists charter, FBOs and customs facilities. International flights still require actual CBP clearance.

### PUJ — Paulding Northwest Atlanta, Georgia: 15,000lb tug capacity is not a runway limit

The [airport's FBO services](https://pauldingairport.com/services.html) state tug services are available for aircraft **up to 15,000lb** and list 24-hour fuel. **Tug capacity is not published runway structural bearing**. Do not reject all heavier aircraft based on a ground-equipment rating. Obtain the separately published pavement/gear rating and actual airline/charter rights.

### BDU — Boulder Municipal, Colorado

The [City of Boulder](https://bouldercolorado.gov/government/departments/airport) confirms the airport has **no commercial airline service** and serves public business, personal and recreational GA. Its [FAQ](https://bouldercolorado.gov/airport-faqs) describes a **24/7 public-use airfield** with 24-hour self-service fuel. Absence of scheduled airlines does not itself establish a total ban on individually licensed charters; confirm the city policy and actual aircraft/operating permissions.

### SYM — Morehead–Rowan County, Kentucky: historical airport-code lineage

The candidate registry retains old **M97** source identification while the active FAA/GPS code is **SYM/KSYM**. Verify the historical alias lineage/coordinates from FAA source before touching airport identity. This is `SOURCE_IDENTITY_RECONCILIATION`; keep the existing immutable airport key and prevent duplicate records.

## Non-admission safety and game safeguards

All 1,929 U.S. expansion airports remain `NOT_APPROVED` for gameplay. No world activation, airport category approval or change to the original **3,140-airport** playable roster was made. Airlinerra has exactly **six classifications: Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub**; external aviation planning 'regional', 'business', 'international' or GA designations are not automatic game categories. A `CONSTRAINT_CONFIRMED_HOLD` is not necessarily a whole-airport closure or permanent rejection; an `EVIDENCE_GAP_HOLD` does not prove there are no commercial charters or cargo flights.

Before actual flights or any future game activation, independently verify **current** runway-specific paved length and declared distances, pavement/aircraft weight ratings, operating NOTAM, owner/tribal commercial permissions, Part121/135/139 compliance as applicable, fuel/ground service, weather/terrain and named operating certificates. This report is research triage, **not aviation flight clearance or gameplay approval**.
