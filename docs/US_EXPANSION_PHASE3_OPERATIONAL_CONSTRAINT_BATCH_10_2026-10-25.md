## Key decisions and safeguards

1. **Erie Municipal EIK:** Town of Erie directly says its noise-abatement guidance is voluntary, not a mandatory flight restriction. The paved runway remains subject to rising terrain, density altitude, FAA rules, certified aircraft performance, and written permission for paid flights.
2. **Dare County MQI:** Its owner confirms paved 05/23 at 4,305 × 100 ft and 17/35 at 3,300 × 75 ft; the shorter secondary does not meet the 1,200m screen, but cannot disqualify main 05/23. Landing distance for each flight direction must still be reconciled before aircraft eligibility.
3. **Newport ONP:** A specific **prior-permission-required** rule applies to air-carrier operations with **more than 30 passenger seats**, with RW34 PAPI unusable sectors. This is an actual legally consequential access issue, so Newport remains held for owner and aircraft authorization.
4. **Harry Clever PHD:** The paved main is nominally **only ~1,204m**. Do not equate nominal 3,950ft strip length with verified 1,200m declared landing distance; advancement only confirms grass crosswind does not close paved main. Hard LDA and aircraft constraints still control.
5. **Littlefield LIU / Menomonie LUM / Little Falls LXL / Marshalltown MIW / North Vernon OVO:** Each has a distinct short secondary and a nominally qualifying paved primary. Secondary runways cannot be substituted for primary geometry or used to reject an otherwise usable main.
6. **Gatlinburg–Pigeon Forge GKT:** The airport operator describes no instrument approaches or commercial flights; tourism descriptions mention private charter. The contradiction and aircraft/owner permission require direct reconciliation. It remains held.
7. **Lebanon Municipal M54:** A 2026 airport committee agenda about lighting/PAPI works is **not** proof completion, nor a current FAA operational approval. It remains held for date-valid construction and lighting clearance.
8. **Miami University OXD:** Governance/airport board operating contracts do not alone constitute a physical runway closure. This clarification is not university/board carrier permission.
9. **Airlinerra policy:** Exactly **six** established airport categories, never four. No category decision, gameplay admission or airport activation occurred. Primary runway must be operational asphalt/concrete ≥1,200m **and have sufficient direction-specific declared landing length**, width, loading, weather, obstacles and flight authorization for its actual aircraft.
