## Principal constraints and classification safeguards

1. **Clearwater Executive (CLW) is NOT under a voluntary curfew.** City of Clearwater municipal code §4.23 prohibits normal departures and arrivals **23:00–07:00**, with specifically limited emergency and manager-approved traffic-reporting departure exceptions. The airport itself advertises a 4,108ft paved 16/34 runway and FBO availability. No game aircraft should be dispatched into prohibited windows. Sources: https://www.myclearwater.com/My-Government/0-City-Departments/Marine-Aviation/Clearwater-Executive-Airport ; https://library.municode.com/fl/clearwater/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=SPAGEOR_CH4AIAI
2. **Chandler Regional (CQB), Oklahoma: never use planned runway expansion as today's runway.** State and city identify current ~4,000x60ft pavement. The proposed extension to 5,000ft/widening to 75ft and runway rehabilitation are scheduled for future construction, beginning in 2027. Current pavement distress, wheel loading and manager consent must be assessed on the existing geometry. Sources: https://oklahoma.gov/aerospace/airports/find-an-airport/chandler-regional.html ; https://oklahoma.gov/aerospace/outreach-advocacy/newsroom/oklahoma_greenlights_520_million_acp.html
3. **Fletcher Field (CKM):** crop-spraying and training movements demand tactical NOTAM and traffic coordination, but no source establishes a permanent closure of the otherwise operational paved main runway. This is a traffic-scope advance only, not passenger/cargo operator authorization.
4. **Camilla–Mitchell County (CXU):** agricultural, forestry and emergency medical helicopter use calls for sequencing/traffic separation, not a blanket paved runway closure. RW08/26 actual usable LDA, pavement/obstacles and owner-approved certificated air carrier remain pending.
5. **Chandler Municipal (CHD), Arizona:** municipal references to active **helicopter charter** must not be misrepresented as a fixed-wing airline schedule or proof of a certificated cargo fleet. Phoenix airspace and distinct runway geometry require type-specific evaluation.
6. **Sparta/Fort McCoy (CMY)** is Army-controlled joint-use; commercial aviation access needs explicit military/civil authority, not just public-use listing. **Beech-like factory or military facilities cannot automatically admit scheduled commercial flights**.
7. **Billy G Ray (CNP)** has poor 55ft-class pavement; **Westwinds (D17)** has only 40ft-class paved width at high elevation. These are meaningful aircraft-width and strength constraints regardless of length.
8. **Cleburne (CPT) and Claxton–Evans (CWV)** have documented displaced approach thresholds. Select **runway-direction declared usable landing distance** (LDA) and aircraft wheel loads rather than raw physical paved length.
9. **Custer County (CUT), Faith (D07), Converse County (DGW), Dillon (DLN), Delta (DTA)** need airplane-specific winter/altitude/weather and owner/charter services checks. FBO fuel is not licensed airline service.
10. The game's hard minimum remains **1,200 metres operational paved asphalt or concrete, with confirmed usable direction-specific LDA**, plus width, aircraft loading, weather/terrain/obstacle performance, active dated NOTAM, fuel/handling, airport owner permission and actual certified commercial operation.
