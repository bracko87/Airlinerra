## Interpretation and control
`independent_review_at` indicates **the completion of a first external airport-specific research pass**, not confirmation that scheduled passenger, passenger charter, cargo or every aircraft type can operate. `operator_evidence_url` is the actual dated source used and `source_research_note` gives the precise finding. If general aviation alone is established, commercial suitability is still unresolved. No game admission status, activation or existing six-game-class final category was changed.

Prioritize follow-up for airports where charter/cargo is unresolved, and give E24, XNX, GCY and height/terrain-sensitive airfields extra attention. For future scale, source-backed reviews should run in 50-airport groups without assigning false commercial approvals or repeating already completed runway screening.
