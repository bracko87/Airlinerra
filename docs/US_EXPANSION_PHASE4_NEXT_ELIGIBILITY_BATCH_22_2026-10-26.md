Published runway-end ID light, pilot-controlled lighting and taxiway-edge lighting outages also require authoritative **date-valid NOTAM confirmation**. Those equipment notices are not themselves proof of permanent runway closure.
- **Operations:** seasonal skydiving and gliders (April–September), banner towing (May–October), wildfire fire-tanker flights (May–September), migrating birds and winter ice/snow. Dedicated large transient parking on south ramp spaces T10–T12, runway condition reports limited on weekends/holidays, FSS/AFIS coordination and Anchorage terminal airspace require detailed flight-specific plans.
- **Fuel/owner:** FAA lists 100LL and Jet A1 on north/south self-service ramps, plus major airframe and engine maintenance. The City of Palmer advertises flightseeing/cargo charter activity, but the **particular proposed player's paid-flight commercial use rights and operator certificate have NOT been established**.
