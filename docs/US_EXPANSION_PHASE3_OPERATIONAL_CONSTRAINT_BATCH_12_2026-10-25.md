## Critical scope, restrictions and policy notes

1. **Willows–Glenn WLW:** County of Glenn airport operator explicitly states two distinct runways: lighted **16/34 4,125 x 100 ft** and unlighted **13/31 3,778 x 60 ft**. Only 16/34 passes the 1,200m physical-length screen. The shorter runway cannot disqualify the main. Source: https://www.countyofglenn.net/government/departments/public-works/airports. Direction-specific declared landing length, runway strength, aircraft fit, current NOTAM, fuel/ground support and written commercial permission still require validation.
2. **Caribou CAR:** City of Caribou directly lists separate asphalt runways **01/19 4,003 x 100 ft** and **11/29 3,017 x 75 ft**, with pilot-controlled lighting. Source: https://www.cariboumaine.org/living-in-caribou/airport/. The second fails the 1,200m screen; main nominal pavement just passes. FAA-derived 2026 remarks show approach obstacles, a 398ft displaced threshold on shorter RW29 and 100LL fuel, but no confirmed Jet A. City also documents international entry/advance CBP notice terms. Commercial/legal and selected airplane access are not approved.
3. **Marysville MYZ:** Official city source explicitly states **NO FUEL AVAILABLE** and **12,500lb maximum airport weight limit**: https://www.cityofmarysvilleks.com/airport. This remains held; do not treat the weight limit as merely advisory or invent Jet A.
4. **Twin County HLX / Gene Snyder K62 / Fillmore FOM:** Published low single-wheel load limits, runway markings, approach aids, trees and high altitude represent real aircraft-specific dispatch constraints. Nominal paved length alone is not clearance.
5. **Morrisville–Stowe MVL:** A 2026 FBO tender/transition creates uncertainty about on-ground staffing, despite earlier Vermont DOT fuel and maintenance listings. Do not presume uninterrupted FBO services.
6. **Granbury GDJ and White County MCX:** Self-service fuel, after-hours callout or fuel-truck appointments do not establish reliable commercial passenger/cargo turnarounds. Airport owner access and certified carrier remain additional gates.
7. **Omak OMK:** Outside fueling and land use require city arrangements. An available administrative form is not permission already granted. Therefore held until approved service/owner terms are verified.
8. **Airlinerra minimum eligibility:** A runway must be operational, paved asphalt or concrete and at least **1,200 metres**. True usable direction-specific LDA, width, loading, obstacles, NOTAM, fuel/handling, weather and owner/certificated operator legality must also pass. Smaller or grass secondaries may not substitute for the selected primary.
9. **Exactly six Airlinerra airport categories** remain mandatory; none assigned or modified. No original 3,140 airports affected and no gameplay activation of expansion cases.
