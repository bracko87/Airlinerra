Jet A after-hours requires advance callout and published remarks say to contact airport manager for a designated public-use ramp.

County anticipates a seasonal pause in November; specific dates and aircraft need verification. Positive charter evidence is preserved; game approval not granted.

**AXS Altus–Quartz Mountain, Oklahoma:** [City airport page](https://www.altusok.gov/page/airport) still displays a runway-light outage, but [airport live feed](https://www.altusok.gov/o/aqmra/live-feed) reports restored runway lights in a newer post. Record as a **source-time conflict**, not a confirmed present-day shutdown of all lighting. Check live FAA notices and owner before night operations.

**ASX John F Kennedy Memorial, Wisconsin:** [FAA remarks](https://www.airnav.com/airport/ASX) require **aircraft over 4,000lb to park on concrete**, not asphalt. That is a ground/stand condition, not an airport-wide gross-weight prohibition.

**AJR Habersham County, Georgia:** runway remains distinct from a reported **Taxiway A segment restriction for aircraft wingspans over 80ft**. Confirm exact segment and taxi routing before matching aircraft.

**ADG Lenawee County and ADT Atwood–Rawlins:** short turf secondary runways do **not** qualify under Airlinerra's paved-runway criterion. Their separate qualifying paved primary runways are not automatically closed.

**AID Anderson, ANE Anoka, AOH Lima Allen County, ASH Nashua, BCT Boca Raton, BAZ New Braunfels, AWM West Memphis:** owner/FBO sources affirm charter, business or freight activity to varying degrees. That positive evidence has been recorded, while each specific operator license, aircraft and game admission remains unresolved.
