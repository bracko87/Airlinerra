## Priority findings from source review

### SVR — South Valley Regional, Utah: U42 to SVR is officially documented

[Salt Lake City Department of Airports' March 2025 newsletter](https://slcairport.com/assets/pdfDocuments/GA-Newsletter/General-Aviation-March-2025-Update.pdf) explicitly says the former **U42** identifier would change to **KSVR on April 17, 2025**. The [FAA-derived October 2026 airport listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KSVR) confirms current **SVR/KSVR**. The candidate's source identification retains KU42. Reconcile old/new codes and coordinates within the existing airport key; **do not create a duplicate or change the game roster**. Local SkyShare FBO advertises private charters and fueling, a positive charter-service lead, but certificate, actual airplane fleet and city permission remain to verify.

### VDF — Tampa Executive, Florida: RW23 has 4,200ft landing distance

[FAA 2026 Chart Supplement](https://aeronav.faa.gov/Upload_313-d/supplements/CS_SE_20260319.pdf) shows physical paved RW05/23 **5,000×100ft**, with **RW23 displaced threshold 800ft and declared LDA 4,200ft**; RW05 LDA is **4,956ft**. Runway 18/36 is **3,219ft** (below Airlinerra's 1,200m paved minimum). Verify individual airplane landing performance, pavement loads and landing direction before any eligibility decision. Charter operations at VDF cannot be inferred from scheduled Tampa International TPA flights.

### VNW — Van Wert County, Ohio: main runway has low structural rating

[FAA 2026 Chart Supplement](https://aeronav.faa.gov/Upload_313-d/supplements/CS_EC_20260319.pdf) lists RW09/27 **5,005×75ft grooved asphalt with S-8 (8,000lb single-wheel) load rating**. Its secondary runway is a **2,558ft turf strip**, below the game's paved runway rule. The paved main is not physically excluded on length, but **an aircraft exceeding the actual load rating cannot be assumed suitable**. Air carrier licensing and owner permissions are separate checks.

### X07 — Lake Wales Municipal, Florida: published no air-taxi service and GA aircraft planning class

The [City of Lake Wales](https://cityoflakewales.com/airport) explicitly reports **no current air-carrier, commuter or air-taxi operations** and defines its FAA general-utility planning role for GA aircraft up to about **12,500lb maximum gross takeoff weight and wingspan under 79ft**. The field also has gliders and skydiving. These airport planning dimensions should not be misrepresented as an unconditional legal ban on any airplane above that size without current runway/owner verification; they require fleet-specific capacity checks. No actual current commercial passenger/cargo flight provider is proven.

### X14 — LaBelle Municipal, Florida: physical runway and landing distances differ

[Florida DOT airport-specific information](https://www.fdot.gov/aviation/airportslist/X14) identifies RW14/32 threshold displacements of approximately **351ft and 129ft** respectively. Actual usable LDA must be verified separately by direction, aircraft type and operating date, rather than qualifying solely from the 5,254ft nominal paved strip.

### UES — Waukesha County, Wisconsin: positive charter with instrument/taxiway conditions

The [Waukesha County airport business directory](https://www.waukeshacounty.gov/public-works/airport/airport-business/) lists **Steins Aircraft Services** as a charter operator. October 2026 FAA-derived records mention runway 10 ILS/PAPI and taxiway issues which should be checked for live NOTAM validity; do **not** declare runway permanently closed. Verify the charter operator's current credentials and aircraft.

### FIN and BYL — code differences under review, not duplicate airports

**FIN Flagler Executive, Florida:** Original candidate registry source KXFL differs from the current [FAA identifier FIN/KFIN](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KFIN). **BYL Williamsburg–Whitley County, Kentucky:** Registry source KW38 differs from [current FAA code BYL/KBYL](https://airnav.com/airport/BYL). The evidence confirms their current identifiers, **not yet the full historical code-transition lineage**. Cross-check official FAA code histories/coordinates before any alias correction. Retain each immutable airport key and all existing gameplay data.

### Other positive-service and operational distinctions

- **VYS Illinois Valley Regional, IL** and **WDR Barrow County, GA:** city and county advertise private charters. Verify actual air-carrier operators and aircraft rather than treating general aviation as charter absent.
- **WVL Waterville, Maine:** city's FY2026 budget identifies **daily UPS freight activity**, but without confirmed aircraft/flight number it could refer to cargo delivered by truck or through another carrier; this is a positive freight research lead, not a fabricated scheduled air-cargo route.
- **VNC Venice, Florida:** city says it does not intend scheduled passenger airlines or large-scale cargo, while general aviation charters/medical/corporate flights remain possible. Preserve this important distinction.
- **UGN Waukegan National, IL:** international customs by appointment is an owner/CBP permission task and not automatic authority to run cross-border flights.
- **W43 Hulett, WY:** elevation/terrain and approximately 12,500lb single-wheel runway capacity are consequential to the selected airframe.
- **U52 Beaver and U69 Duchesne, Utah:** high field elevations, narrow or short-margin runways, and limited/no turbine fuel/repair support require airplane-specific assessment.
- **VGT North Las Vegas:** event-specific prior permission and busy Class B/GA airspace require date-scoped checks, not blanket permanent closure.
- **FIN Flagler Executive:** tower hours and two-runway layout must be checked independently of any identity reconciliation.
