## Non-negotiable eligibility safeguards

- Only a qualifying operational paved runway (asphalt or concrete) with **at least 1,200m**, adequate **direction-specific declared usable landing length** and appropriate width, pavement-bearing and aircraft performance may eventually qualify. Turf, short or closed secondary runways never inherit primary properties.
- Strictly separate historical or expired runway notices, pilot-requested voluntary noise guidance, airfield traffic, staffing and commercial-operator permission. A limited secondary/runway-specific issue is not always an airport-wide shutdown.
- Airframe-specific wheel loading, hot/high performance, terrain, approach aids and **current dated NOTAM** must be checked before service approval. A first-pass physical pass does not authorize actual air-carrier operations.
- Preserve all **six** existing Airlinerra category definitions. This batch makes no category assignments. All 20 resolution rows are `NOT_APPROVED`.
- None of the 1,929 Phase 3 selection records was deleted. No entry in the original playable 3,140 cohort was modified by this batch.
- Avoid relying solely on the most permissive archived source when different information conflicts. Example: Independence Municipal historical sources disagree about how Jet A is supplied; confirm airport-level fuel availability for the actual schedule, rather than treating available Jet A as automatic 24-hour service.
