## Important source findings
- **L35 Big Bear City**: airport authority explicitly says **no charter service currently**; ambulance and scenic helicopter services are separate.
- **LOZ London-Corbin**: airport owner says **no charter service currently**, though private and business aircraft use the field.
- **UES Waukesha**: county lists charter provider Stein's; October 2026 NOTAMs indicate temporary instrument/taxiway restrictions.
- **SUA Stuart Witham**: county lists charter FBOs and voluntary **23:00-07:00** nighttime curfew with emergency exemptions.
- **OXC Waterbury-Oxford**: Connecticut authority lists Clay Lacy, Richmor and Tradewind charter service companies.
- **SPA, FDK, PSF, LWC, LVM, HWD**: airport owners/authorities explicitly report charter or on-demand aviation; PSF additionally reports air-freight charter.
- **VNC Venice**: city states airport will not offer scheduled airline or large-scale cargo services; GA charter activity is distinct.
- **FFM Fergus Falls**: city confirms there is **no scheduled airline service**.
- **BTY Beatty**: aviation listing says fuel is **temporarily unavailable** as of the source; independently recheck before any game operation.
- **1U7, 38U**: high altitude, very limited fuel/repairs, aircraft performance constraints.
- **U96 Cal Black Memorial** is Halls Crossing UT, while **U69** is Duchesne Municipal: preserve separate airport identities.
