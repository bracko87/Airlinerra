## Specific operating-rule distinctions confirmed

**Aurora Municipal (ARR), Illinois.** [FAA airport handbook, updated June 23, 2026](https://www.faa.gov/flight_deck/arr) says RW18/36 closed indefinitely; the currently laid out paved **09/27** and **15/33** remain separate operating runways. Aircraft taxi/ATC hotspots and live NOTAM still matter. Do not close the full airport merely because 18/36 is unavailable.

**Childress (CDS), Texas.** [FAA airport remarks](https://www.flightaware.com/resources/airport/KCDS/remarks) say poorly maintained RW04/22 closed indefinitely and concrete/asphalt RW18/36 is separate and retains lights, some nonstandard. Check 18/36 declared LDA, weight and owner permission before any later admission.

**Lenawee County (ADG), Michigan.** [County operator](https://lenawee.mi.us/425/Airport) confirms about **5,000ft** of paved main runway and a separate grass strip. Winter grass closure cannot be inherited by paved operations.

**Gwinner (GWR), North Dakota.** [September 2026 FAA Chart Supplement transcription](https://tallyaero.com/atlas/chart-supplement/NC_339/) separately publishes paved **RW16/34 5,000×75ft concrete**, with Jet A and avgas, and **2,991ft turf RW06/24 closed in winter**. Winter snow removal, wildlife, pavement wheel ratings and actual legal carrier remain separate gates.

**Perry (PRO), Iowa.** [Iowa DOT current airport directory](https://opmapps.iowadot.gov/aviation/airports/PRO) confirms **RW14/32 5,500×100ft concrete**, plus nonqualifying **2,322ft turf 04/22**. Some older city copy states 4,000ft; use modern surveyed runway measurements and verify skydiving/airport permission.

**Red Oak (RDK), Iowa.** [Iowa DOT](https://secure.iowadot.gov/aviation/data_driven/publications/mapinfosys/AirportDirectory.aspx?FAACode=RDK) lists **5,100×75ft concrete 05/23**, separate seasonally closed turf **13/31**, and short secondary concrete **17/35**, about 2,901ft. Only the long primary is screened as potentially suitable.

**Wendell H Ford (CPF), Kentucky.** [FAA approach notices](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KCPF) include RW06/24 night/circling limitations and specific takeoff/approach procedures. Primary **14/32** remains distinct, but its airplane-specific terrain, obstacle and runway-direction limits still require verification.

**Mobridge (MBG), South Dakota.** [State 2026–28 airport directory](https://dot.sd.gov/media/q3tjuxzr/2026-2028-south-dakota-airport-directory-web-version.pdf) describes a 4,410ft-class paved runway distinct from winter-closed turf. Do not conflate them.

**Livermore (LVK), California.** [City official noise guidance](https://www.livermoreca.gov/departments/public-works/airport/airport-noise-information) says night-flying restraint **22:00–06:00 is voluntary**, not a binding all-aircraft curfew. Noise best practices remain necessary; airlines/operator/airplane fit and any actual binding restrictions must be checked.

**Lincoln Karl Harder (LHM), California.** [City airport information](https://www.lincolnca.gov/business-and-development/airport/) identifies temporary limited **monthly tie-down parking**, not proof of a closed six-thousand-foot paved runway. Current taxi/ramp closure/parking, aircraft loads and owner commercial rights remain.
