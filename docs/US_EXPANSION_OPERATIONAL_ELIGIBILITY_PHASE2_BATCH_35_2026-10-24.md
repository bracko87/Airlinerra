## Priority research and corrections

### H04 — Vinita Municipal, Oklahoma, not Weatherford

The [City of Vinita FAA information](https://cityofvinita.com/191/FAA-Information) establishes H04 at Vinita, Oklahoma and publishes its physical coordinates and operating runway. The [Oklahoma Airport System Plan](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/oac/documents/airports-airport-system-plan/OASP-Technical-Report.pdf) explicitly distinguishes **Vinita H04** from **Weatherford Thomas P. Stafford OJA**. The earlier research text erroneously associated H04 with Weatherford. Flagged as `SOURCE_IDENTITY_RECONCILIATION` so the false linkage can be corrected **without mutating the airport's immutable key or creating a duplicate**, then redo H04-specific airline/ground capability.

### JPX — East Hampton, New York: historic HTO-to-JPX change

The [Town of East Hampton's 2026 airport operational-changes document](https://ehamptonny.gov/DocumentCenter/View/15600/East-Hampton-Town-Airport-Operational-Changes-DRAFT-DGEIS-Scoping-Document) explicitly states the airport identifier changed from **HTO to JPX in May 2022**. Candidate registry source code still reads KHTO while current GPS is KJPX. Preserve the one physical airport, confirm current FAA alias/coordinates and separately validate current town noise, aircraft-fee/curfew proposals versus enforceable operating restrictions. Airport operator reports on-field charter services and a seasonal tower; neither automatically means scheduled airline permission.

### HTL — Roscommon County–Blodgett Memorial, Michigan: snow rule affects turf

The [county airport owner](https://www.roscommoncounty.net/158/Airport/) and [current FAA-derived runway record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HTL) distinguish **RW09/27 — 4,000 × 75ft paved asphalt** from **RW18/36 — 2,200 × 100ft turf**. The rule **closed when snow covered except ski-equipped aircraft** applies to the **short turf RW18/36**, not inherently to the separate paved qualifying main runway. Check current snow NOTAM separately for RW09/27. Fuel source entries may differ: confirm actual inventory with the FBO rather than assuming all grades continuously available.

### GHM — Centerville, Tennessee: threshold reduces landing length

The [FAA airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/GHM) lists a physical 4,002ft-class runway with approximately **250ft displaced threshold on RW20**. The 1,200m minimum pavement threshold is not enough to infer equal declared landing length in every direction. Check actual LDA and aircraft performance.

### H79 — Eldon Model Airpark, Missouri: published approach-aid outages

[October 2026 airport NOTAM listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/H79) reports PAPIs and REILs out on both runway approaches. These notices are date-sensitive aids/approach restrictions, not proof the pavement itself is closed. Verify latest FAA notices and usable approach procedures.

### GOO — Nevada County, California: positive on-demand charter leads

[Nevada County Airport](https://www.nevadacountyca.gov/149/Airport) confirms **no scheduled commercial passenger service** while listing **Alpine Aviation and Centurion** among aviation/charter businesses. Preserve these commercial-service leads and verify operator certificates/actual airframes before considering gameplay use.

### GPM — Grand Prairie, Texas: freight loading is not flying freight

[Grand Prairie city airport](https://www.gptx.org/Departments/Airport) markets general/corporate flying, FBO and cargo-handling facilities on a 4,000 × 75ft runway. Cargo ground-handling capability is **not independent evidence of an actual scheduled air cargo route**.

### HAI — Three Rivers, Michigan: separate runways

The [City of Three Rivers](https://www.threeriversmi.org/city-departments/municipal-airport/) publishes a 4,000 × 75ft paved main RW09/27 and a **2,720ft crosswind RW05/23**. The shorter runway is below Airlinerra's 1,200m paved criterion and must not inherit the main runway's aircraft capacity or disqualify the whole airfield.

### HYW — Horry County, South Carolina: aircraft weight limit

Conway–Horry County has a 4,401 × 75ft concrete runway with roughly **12,500lb single-wheel bearing**; verify correct current loading by aircraft and runway before any assigned commercial fleet.
