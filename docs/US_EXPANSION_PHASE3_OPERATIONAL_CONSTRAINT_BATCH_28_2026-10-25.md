## Important findings and protected admission gates

- **Cumberland Municipal UBE:** Official City of Cumberland source defines **max runway aircraft weight 12,500lb**, separate seasonal **turf 18/36**, and only **100LL/Swift UL94** self-serve fuel. That aircraft maximum is a binding load rule, not a landing fee. No Jet A was established. Airport remains held. Source: https://cityofcumberland.net/airport/
- **Waukegan National UGN:** Current airport services page says CBP customs processing requires **advance notice** and weekday/weekend appointment windows; North Shore Jet Center has Jet A/ground handling. This is a flight-specific customs requirement, NOT a permanently closed runway. The airport advances only for that scope distinction, without automatic carrier rights. Sources: https://waukeganairport.com/services/ and https://waukeganairport.com/services/customs/
- **Troutdale TTD:** Port of Portland confirms **one paved runway**, FAA contract tower, GA training and helicopter/airplane scenic tours. Those activities need separation but are not themselves runway closure, nor proof that scheduled flights at nearby PDX belong to Troutdale. Source: https://portofportland.com/TTD
- **Troy Municipal TOI, Scott City TQK and Aurora State UAO:** Existing military, medevac, agricultural, training and business traffic cannot alone establish a permanent airfield closure or another airline's certified authority. They advance only for the traffic-versus-closure distinction.
- **Mackay U62:** The October FAA-based remark reports very rough, uneven chip-seal asphalt, poor markings and a RW12 powerline obstacle. Severe pavement and high-elevation aircraft performance make this a continuing hold regardless of nominal runway length.
- **Stanford Field U12:** Physical **4500x50ft** runway has displaced thresholds **300ft and 540ft** at ~4,966ft elevation, requiring exact per-direction usable landing distances, runway width/load and hot/high airplane calculations before a type can qualify.
- **Tehachapi TSP:** The nominal ~1231m pavement is too close to the 1200m minimum to assume usable LDA with mountain obstacle, wind or threshold restrictions.
- **Raleigh Executive TTA:** Published **102,000lb dual-wheel** runway figure is NOT also a single-wheel or airplane gross-weight qualification. The proposed aircraft's gear and declared landing distances still govern.
- **Miami Executive TMB:** Historical **2022 pavement condition values** do not constitute current 2026 safety approval. Airport has distinct runways with independent approach and aircraft requirements.
- **All 20 remain NOT APPROVED for gameplay.** Airlinerra has exactly **six** predefined categories and no final categories were assigned. A future eligible airplane/airport pairing requires an operating asphalt/concrete runway with **at least 1,200 metres of published usable LDA for the selected direction**, sufficient pavement width/load, terrain and weather performance, current NOTAM, confirmed fuel/emergency/ground support, written owner commercial permission and a certificated carrier/operator.
