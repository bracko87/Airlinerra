## Rules for follow-up and gameplay

Every source review is **first-pass research**, not a finding that commercial flights are currently scheduled, that a particular Part 135/121 carrier is authorized, that an aircraft has sufficient takeoff/landing performance, or that the airport can be admitted to the game. Airport-operating rights and aircraft-specific compatibility require additional checks.

Use only Airlinerra's six approved gameplay categories: **Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub**. FAA Part 139 Classes I–IV, state system classes, and airport names such as "Regional" are independent regulatory/planning labels and do not determine these six categories. Keep all new airports `NOT_APPROVED`; do not alter or activate the existing 3,140 original airport entries.
