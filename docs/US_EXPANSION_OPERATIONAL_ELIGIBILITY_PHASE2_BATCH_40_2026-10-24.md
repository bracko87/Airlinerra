## Special decisions to retain

**SFF Felts Field** — [Spokane Airport Authority](https://www.spokaneairports.com/felts-field/at-felts-field/) lists SeaPort Airlines commuter service to Boeing Field and PVTAir charter flights. [King County](https://kingcounty.gov/en/dept/executive-services/transit-transportation-roads/airport/news-announcements/20260304-seaport-service-spokane) announced the Spokane route from March 9, 2026. This is positive commercial-service evidence; verify active flights, certificates, airplane suitability and airport owner permission rather than recording commercial flying absent.

**LOU Bowman Field** — [Airport owner](https://www.flylouisville.com/corporate/bowman-field/) reports 4,356ft paved RW06/24, but [FAA runway data](https://www.airnav.com/airport/LOU) report declared landing distances of **3,469ft RW06** and **3,856ft RW24**, both shorter than 1,200m. Maintain a direction-specific operational hold and distinguish from Louisville SDF.

**Z84 Clear** — [Alaska DOT](https://dot.alaska.gov/nreg/denaliairportstudy/files/daaps-Task3Inventory-ExistingConditions.pdf) describes the field as virtually unusable due to surrounding restricted airspace; FAA remarks warn of R-2206 and high-energy radio emissions. Obtain current ATC/owner clearance. Do not infer unconditional runway physical closure.

**AFE Kake** — Published roughly 1,000ft RW29 threshold displacement, 12,500lb aircraft gross-weight limit except with prior permission, terrain/wildlife and irregular maintenance; assess LDA and aircraft/owner permission individually.

**SWD Seward** — [Alaska DOT project](https://dot.alaska.gov/creg/sewardairport/) describes repeatedly flooded RW13/31 and a future project to close it and extend shorter RW16/34. A proposal does not mean the runway has already closed; check actual NOTAM and runway as-built dimensions.

**UKI Ukiah** — City historical threshold information differs from [current FAA geometry](https://www.airnav.com/airport/UKI). Reconcile runway dimensions and binding no-jet-departure 22:00–07:00 local restriction rather than combining old and new measurements without evidence.
