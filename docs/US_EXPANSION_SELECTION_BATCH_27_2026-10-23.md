## High-impact findings
- **AID Anderson Regional IN**: airport authority says charter traffic occurs despite absence of scheduled commercial flights. Source: https://www.cityofanderson.com/123/Anderson-Regional-Airport
- **GMU Greenville Downtown SC**: airport operator directly lists two named on-site charter/air-taxi providers: Doubleday Aviation and Special Services Corporation. Source: https://www.greenvilledowntownairport.com/services
- **X07 Lake Wales FL**: city says **no current air-carrier, commuter or air-taxi operations** and describes a 12,500lb design role for small GA aircraft. Source: https://cityoflakewales.com/airport
- **ONP Newport OR**: official FAA remark requires **prior permission for airline operations above 30 passenger seats**. The airport advertises charter services; keep individual route and type suitability separate. Sources: https://www.airnav.com/airport/KONP and https://cdd.newportoregon.gov/dept/onp/
- **HKS Hawkins Field MS**: public GA airport operated by Jackson authority, with based PHI Air Medical and Pafford operations and Mississippi Army National Guard; this is civilian-plus-military, not a military-only airport. Source: https://jmaa.com/hawkins-field/
- **GNG Gooding ID**: city page lists historical 4,135ft length, but **FAA October 2026 gives 5,371ft/1,637m**, matching pinned physical screen. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/GNG
- **RYA Wray CO**: current FAA code RYA, formerly 2V5; runway 5,399x75ft asphalt; 100LL only. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/RYA
- **40U Manila UT**: high elevation 6,179ft, unattended, winter runway surface reports intermittent; **not Roosevelt 74V**. Source: https://airnav.com/airport/40U
- **1B1 Columbia County NY**: customs landing rights and based Richmor FBO; customs rights must not be interpreted as scheduled airline authorization. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/1B1
- **6S0 Big Timber MT**: Oct 2026 FAA airport page says paved runway GOOD; a 2024 Montana pavement PCI report calls runway sections FAIR. **Hold physical-condition reconciliation**, not an unquestioned GOOD/EXCELLENT field. FAA https://www.airnav.com/airport/6S0 ; state PCI https://www.mdt.mt.gov/other/webdata/External/Aero/AviationSysPlan/2024/6S0%20-%20Big%20Timber%20-%202024%20PCI%20Report.pdf
- **CTB Cut Bank MT**: international airport-of-entry with customs prior-notification procedures, high-altitude and severe-cold operations. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/KCTB
- **X14 LaBelle FL**: FDOT/FAA report runway threshold displacements (351ft and 129ft) and separately reduced landing distances; aircraft takeoff/landing suitability cannot rely solely on gross runway length. Source: https://www.fdot.gov/aviation/airportslist/X14
- **LVK Livermore CA**: city publishes voluntary **22:00–06:00** jet flight restraint, not an absolute closure. Source: https://www.livermoreca.gov/departments/public-works/airport/airport-faqs
- **WWD Cape May NJ**: authority says GA airport supports charter flights within its annual operations, but no scheduled passenger airline established. Source: https://www.capemayairport.com/
- **4A6** is Scottsboro Word Field, *not Guntersville* FAA **8A1**. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/4A6
