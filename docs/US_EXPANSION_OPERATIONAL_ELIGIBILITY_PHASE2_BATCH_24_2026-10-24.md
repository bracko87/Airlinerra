## Major airport-specific findings

**OCH — Nacogdoches A L Mangham Jr Regional, Texas.** The [city notice dated August 25, 2026](https://www.nactx.us/634/Airport) states the airport reopened after major runway work but RW18 has a **temporary 1,000-foot displaced threshold**, with taxiway restrictions and the ILS unavailable pending works. [October NOTAM listings](https://flyq.seattleavionics.com/Atlas/frmAirports.aspx?Country=US&FAA=OCH&ICAO=KOCH) describe displacement and construction restrictions. Check current notice expiry and actual runway LDA; do not misstate this as a permanent whole-airport closure.

**ONP — Newport Municipal, Oregon.** [FAA-derived remarks](https://www.flightaware.com/resources/airport/KONP/remarks) specify **prior permission for air-carrier flights with more than 30 passenger seats**, plus sector restrictions on RW34 PAPI. This is type-specific access, not proof of a blanket charter prohibition.

**MVN — Mount Vernon Outland, Illinois.** City material describes corporate/charter/cargo aviation and historical Part139 capabilities, while the [FAA Part139 certification list effective September 3, 2026](https://www.faa.gov/airports/airport_safety/part139_cert/part_139_airport_certification_status_list) prompted an inactive-certificate discrepancy in retained airport research. Treat it as a **certification reconciliation issue**, not proof the runway is closed.

**ORG — Orange County, Texas.** The [owner's published airport statement](https://www.co.orange.tx.us/departments/OrangeCountyAirport) says the airport does **not accommodate commercial airline traffic**. Clarify scheduled-carrier versus certificated air-taxi/cargo permissions directly with owner; private and corporate GA continue.

**OXC — Waterbury–Oxford, Connecticut.** [Connecticut Airport Authority](https://ctairports.org/airports/waterbury-oxford/services/) names **Clay Lacy, Richmor and Tradewind** as charter providers. This is positive commercial-aviation evidence, pending flight/operator certificate and aircraft-specific approval; do not misclassify all paid flying as absent.

**MUT — Muscatine, Iowa.** City labels the airport noncommercial but its FBO advertises charter. Reconcile city usage of 'noncommercial' with approved Part135 charter operations before assigning route eligibility.

**OVO — North Vernon, Indiana.** Long paved main **5,002ft** versus shorter paved crosswind **2,730ft**: the shorter is below Airlinerra's 1,200m criterion; it does **not** make the separate main runway ineligible.

**OZA — Ozona, Texas.** Local materials report **70,000lb** limitation. Obtain wheel/gear-specific current published limits before screening heavy aircraft.

**P03, P04 — Arizona.** Cochise College lacks confirmed FBO/fuel; Bisbee lists only avgas and narrow paved runway. Check fuel, ground support, high-desert performance and owner rights rather than assuming both airports closed.
