## Scope and safeguards

Phase 1 had completed airport-specific FAA and owner evidence research for all 1,929 U.S. expansion candidates. Phase 2 is a separate operational eligibility **source triage**: runway and aircraft restrictions, facilities/terrain, carrier-authorization evidence, and airport identifier inconsistencies. A source-reported constraint or missing license is a **hold for further review**, not necessarily a permanent exclusion or a legal prohibition.

Assessment storage: `airlinerra.airport_operational_triage` references immutable keys in `airlinerra.airport_commercial_verification`. Every individual case has its finding, source URL(s), source effective date, specific follow-up, review timestamp and assessor. Existing Phase 1 notes are retained rather than overwritten. Most source observations derive from the October 2026 NASR cycle and research on October 23; several high-impact issues were checked against recent airport-owner or FAA-derived web pages on October 24. When operational facts are time-sensitive, check current NOTAMs and owner terms before game adoption.

## Critical source findings

### CPK — Chesapeake Regional (VA): legitimate charter but not shipment handling

The [airport's official flight services page](https://www.chesapeakeairport.com/flight-service/) explicitly lists **Jet Air Charter, an FAA Part 135 operator using Learjet 45 aircraft**, along with FBO/flight-training businesses. At the same time, the airport itself **states that it does not process shipments of any kind**. This is *affirmative charter-service evidence* and a separate restriction affecting cargo handling—not evidence that no commercial activity takes place and not unlimited permission for scheduled airlines or freight. [Runway/source listing](https://www.chesapeakeairport.com/airport-data/) gives approximately **5,500×100ft asphalt**. Confirm current operator certificate, specific airframe/route, current cargo policy and game-owner permission separately.

### CWV — Claxton–Evans County (GA): 500ft displacement, declared landing distance

The [FAA-derived current runway listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/CWV) for **RW10/28** lists **5,151×75ft asphalt**, **30,000lb single-wheel**, and **500ft displaced RW10 threshold**. Consequently, RW10 **LDA is 4,651ft**, while opposite RW28 **LDA is 5,151ft**. The [FAA Chart Supplement](https://aeronav.faa.gov/afd/22JAN2026/SE_211_22JAN2026.pdf) also confirms those direction-specific declared distances. This does not make the full airport ineligible—the shorter LDA is still above the 1,200m baseline screen—but aircraft-specific takeoff/landing performance is required.

### D60 — Tioga (ND): former FBO shut down in 2024, not the airport runway

The [former provider's own August 29, 2024 statement](https://www.tiogaaero.com/news.html) documents suspension of **Tioga Aero Center fixed-base operations** at D60. Its other old service pages may remain online and must not be taken as evidence of a functioning FBO in October 2026. A **replacement FBO/fueling arrangement is unverified**. The provider closing does not mean the airport runway itself closed. Manager verification is required.

### DVN — Davenport Municipal (IA): real charter and air freight business

The [City of Davenport](https://www.davenportiowa.com/services/public_works/airport) confirms **Jet Air Inc** among tenants and identifies on-field services including charter, air ambulance and **air freight**. Distinguish genuine positive commercial-use evidence from the unresolved question of which aircraft, certifications and exact routes could be added to Airlinerra. Do not infer an ordinary scheduled passenger airline from this activity.

### E14 — Ohkay Owingeh (NM): tribal owner/access and unclear threshold remarks

The [FAA-derived airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/E14) identifies **Ohkay Owingeh Tribal Council** ownership, an unattended airfield at approximately **5,760ft elevation**, wildlife and an access-controlled ground entrance. There are contradictory current/legacy comments about **RW16 threshold displacement being present versus removed**. Avoid publishing ground-entry credentials or relying on the ambiguous old wording; get current NASR/manager-confirmed runway LDA and commercial-access permission directly.
