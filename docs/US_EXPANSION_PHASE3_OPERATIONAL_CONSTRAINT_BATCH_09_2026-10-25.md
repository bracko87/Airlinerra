## Audit safeguards and important counterexamples

1. **Exactly six Airlinerra categories** remain the only classification model; this batch makes no assignments and does not alter the original 3,140-airport roster.
2. The physical runway screen requires **operational asphalt or concrete ≥1,200m**, but category and aircraft permissions also depend on **actual directional declared LDA**, runway width and load, weather, terrain and current NOTAM.
3. **Jerry Tyler Memorial (3TR)** has a qualifying main paved runway and a separate short damaged secondary, so the latter cannot automatically reject the main. Yet RW15 threshold displacement, runway S-25 and obstacle notices still need type-specific verification.
4. **Blairsville (DZJ)** has a nominal 5,004ft paved runway but directional landing lengths reported 4,017ft for RW08 and 4,319ft for RW26; slope, terrain and obstruction checks are pending. Do not substitute strip length for LDA.
5. **Keystone Heights (42J)** has discrepant published threshold-displacement information from state and airport sources. Kept held until currently authoritative FAA declared landing distances reconcile.
6. **Bourland Field (50F)** has a documented aircraft limit of 12,500lb maximum gross; this is an operational prohibition, not voluntary noise advice. Kept held.
7. **Greenville Municipal (3B1)** advertises self-service Jet A/100LL and FBO; dated taxiway construction notices, winter conditions, usable LDA and lawful commercial operator are not yet cleared.
8. Advancement means an activity restriction or assigned public ramp is not itself evidence of a permanently closed runway. It does **not** imply access to airline services and must never set gameplay approval.
