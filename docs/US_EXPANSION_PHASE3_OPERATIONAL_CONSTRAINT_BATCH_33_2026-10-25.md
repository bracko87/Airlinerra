## Key safety distinctions and research findings

- **Jones Field (F00), Wilbarger County (F05), Fort Morgan Municipal (FMM):** The respective airport/city owners document seasonal crop-dusting alongside business/general aviation. Seasonal aerial agriculture needs actual traffic/NOTAM separation but does **not** independently prove a permanent physical paved-runway closure. Advancements only clarify that scope. Each still requires aircraft LDA/gear/airframe compatibility, lawful paid-service permission and named licensed provider.
- **Rohnerville (FOT), California:** Published landing fees and exemptions are economic conditions, not a prohibited airplane gross-weight limit or total airport closure. Runway is only ~4,004ft physically (~1,220m), making usable published LDA essential. Coastal fog and unattended services remain separate holds until resolved. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/FOT
- **Northern Aroostook Regional (FVE), Maine:** Customs landing-rights procedures exist, with Jet A and avgas in published records. Entry rights require advance CBP clearance and winter dispatch; they are not a physical runway closure and do not authorize an unspecified cross-border carrier. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/FVE
- **Fort Worth Spinks (FWS), Texas:** The city aviation authority describes corporate/general aviation and charter. Larger aircraft can require airport-specific permission, and positive charter activity is not unrestricted heavy-jet capacity or a new operator certificate. Source: https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/aviation/spinks/general-aviation
- **Delaware Coastal (GED), Delaware:** FAA documentation lists customs landing rights and FBO normally staffed around 08:00–18:00, along with wildlife concerns. CBP slots and ground response are real procedural requirements but not a permanent runway closure. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/GED
- **Rusk County (RFI), Texas:** County explicitly states its airport is **not a customs landing-rights airport**. A courtesy car needing 48h request is separate from commercial flight or CBP authority, which remains held. Source: https://www.ruskcountytx.gov/page/rusk.Airport.Facilities
- **Fort Bridger (FBR) and Carrizozo Municipal (F37):** High-altitude/mountain weather, payload, obstacle climb and aircraft-specific declared distances remain unresolved and legally permissible passenger/cargo operator not verified.
- **Fillmore County (FKA), Frankfort Dow (FKS) and Bigfork (FOZ):** Winter maintenance, unattended or appointment-only FBO support, and near-threshold physical runway lengths do not amount to qualified safe runway/certified commercial service.
- **French Lick (FRH):** FAA airport remarks caution approach turbulence and limited attended hours. Aircraft-specific approach weather and emergency handling remain needed.
- **Fort Scott (FSK):** FAA source reports runway-adjacent terrain drop-off and no published on-field airframe/powerplant repair. Fuel and pavement do not remove obstacle, landing and operational support restrictions.
- **Montgomery County (GAI) and Brookhaven Calabro (HWV):** Airport 24h or urban GA access does not waive actual published approach/noise and owner authorization rules. HWV is in Shirley, NY, not East Hampton.
- **Granite Falls (GDB) and Tulia–Swisher County (I06):** Unattended or intermittently attended fields require verified present turbine fuel, winter/high-plains weather response, airframe rating and carrier.
- **Final admission guardrail:** Airlinerra uses **exactly six existing categories**; this batch assigns **none**. Game approval requires a verified operating asphalt/concrete landing direction with at least **1,200m usable published LDA**, adequate wheel loading and width, aircraft climb/weather/obstacles, current NOTAM/FICON, available fuel/emergency handling, written owner commercial permission and relevant certificated airline/charter/cargo operator. Advancing a narrowly interpreted restriction never constitutes a gameplay approval.
