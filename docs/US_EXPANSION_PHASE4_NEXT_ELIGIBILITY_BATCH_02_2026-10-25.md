## Important findings and verification distinctions

### Jerry Tyler Memorial 3TR — Runway 15 fails the nominal landing-distance screen

October 2026 FAA-derived runway listing confirms the qualifying-length **15/33 asphalt runway at 4,100x75ft**, **S-25,000lb** wheel bearing, but the **RW15 threshold is displaced 300ft**. That leaves only **3,800ft (approximately 1,158m) of landing pavement** in that direction, below Airlinerra's **1,200m** cutoff, independent of the presence of an unverified published LDA. The other direction (RW33) has no published displaced threshold in the available record and **must be checked independently** for actual usable LDA, obstacle clearance and runway/airplane strength, with restricted PAPI approach sectors. The separate asphalt 04/22 is **3,316ft**, under the game's basic length threshold. The present conclusion is **direction-specific**, not a permanent closure of the whole airport. Sources: https://www.airnav.com/airport/3TR and https://www.flightaware.com/resources/airport/3TR/runway/15/33

### Hart Airport 3R4 — very low structural runway rating

October FAA listing reports **12/30 4,402x75ft fair asphalt**, a **6,000lb single-wheel** pavement capacity and tree obstacles on both approaches. Airport remarks require contacting the airport manager for designated public ramp space, and list self-service avgas with no aircraft or engine repair service. Ramp allocation is administrative, but **S-6,000lb** is a genuine aircraft/load restriction. A substantial fraction of commercial aircraft will be ineligible until a named type and its landing-gear loading are positively assessed. No published directional LDA, written city permit or licensed air carrier is verified. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/3R4
