## Why this phase is separate

Completing a facility-source research note does **not** certify that an airport can support an airline-management game's routes. A separate phase must assess: usable paved runway; actual operating constraints, displaced thresholds, pavement load; permissions of owner, military/tribal or airpark association; air-carrier/charter/cargo certification; and realistic aircraft performance. Statuses in the triage table only record independently rechecked **constraints and holds**, **never game approval**.

The `airport_operational_triage` table was added by Supabase migration `create_airport_operational_triage_gate`; it includes immutable airport foreign keys, constrained statuses, priority, source URL(s), source effective date, issue, required next action and UTC check time. It has RLS enabled, no anonymous/authenticated grants or policies. The original research ledger and all game-facing tables were left unchanged.
