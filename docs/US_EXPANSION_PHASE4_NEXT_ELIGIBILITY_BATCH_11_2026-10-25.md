RW33's separate 144ft displaced threshold leaves **4,956ft (~1,511m)** nominal pavement, but authoritative LDA and 50ft airplane-width/loading must be verified. It is a **privately owned skydiving/ultralight airport**, FAA instructs avoidance of the airport and east adjacent overflight airspace, and the listed fuel is **for emergency use only**, not a scheduled Jet A turnaround. No airport-wide gameplay authorization follows from consideration of RW33. [FAA 2026 data](https://www.airnav.com/airport/L65).

The permitted westbound landing direction's official LDA and terrain performance have not been established. Jet A1+ and avgas card service are available but no on-field aircraft/engine repairs listed. [FAA 2026 runway restriction](https://www.airnav.com/airport/LAM).
