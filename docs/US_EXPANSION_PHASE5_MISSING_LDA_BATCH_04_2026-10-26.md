### Key interpretations

**AVO / Avon Park:** RW23's nominal landing pavement is 5,374 - 1,044 = 4,330 feet. This passes the nominal minimum but **does not prove the FAA published LDA**. The other runway 10/28 is only 3,844 feet and therefore fails on gross dimensions. Airport beacon OTS and after-hours/on-call conditions remain separate restrictions.

**BNO / Burns:** The AirNav page available for this review is FAA-effective 03 September 2026; primary 12/30 5,101 feet, crosswind 03/21 4,600 feet; RW03 with 600-foot displaced threshold leaves 4,000 feet nominal. The narrow distance margin requires updated official cycle LDA. Do not treat a displacement-derived estimate as declared LDA.

**C80 / New Coalinga:** The 5,000-foot 12/30 asphalt runway is listed operational but its centerline crack seal/marking limitations and RW30 VGSI outage need checking. The separate 2,471-foot 01/19 is explicitly closed due to pavement failure. Do not automatically disqualify the airport on that secondary runway; neither direction of 12/30 has an official LDA confirmed here.

**BNL / Barnwell:** 17/35 remains listed in good condition, whereas 05/23 has cracks, raveling/FOD, and an aircraft restriction of 12,500lb or less. Restrictions tied to one runway do not automatically apply to the other.

**ASX / Ashland:** Aircraft over 4,000lb must park on concrete; that is not the runway's 40,000lb single-wheel bearing limit. 

**C29, C75, CAR, CCY, BST:** Primary physical runway lengths are barely above 1,200 metres. Requiring official LDA, selected airplane takeoff/landing performance and pavement strength is particularly important.
