## High-confidence operating/identity distinctions

### FMN — Farmington, New Mexico: current scheduled airline verified

[Official Four Corners Regional Airport](https://www.farmingtonnm.gov/172/Airport) confirms **daily United Express service operated by SkyWest** between FMN and **DEN**, using 50-seat CRJ200 aircraft. The service began in May 2025; a [2026 contemporaneous passenger report](https://www.tricityrecordnm.com/enplanements-increase-at-farmington-airport/) documents continued operations. This is **real scheduled service**, not an unproven airport airline label. No automatic game admission or automatic suitability for every aircraft type follows from the schedule.

### JKA/GUF — Gulf Shores, Alabama: operating Allegiant service, separate airport-code systems

[City of Gulf Shores July 2026 report](https://www.gulfshoresal.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1271) describes active Allegiant passenger service and a planned terminal expansion; [2025–26 city destination announcements](https://www.gulfshoresal.gov/m/NewsFlash/Archive/Item/1115?arcId=2170) identify seasonal routes. **GUF** is the airline/IATA code and **JKA** is the FAA local code, not two separate airports. Preserve immutable `KJKA` registry identity and check operating aircraft/owner rights before game admission.

### MKT — Mankato, Minnesota: airline ticket with ground transfer is not scheduled flight

[Official Mankato airport passenger page](https://mankatoairport.com/passenger-%2F-crew-info) explicitly confirms the MKT–MSP/Sun Country ticket is fulfilled by **Landline road shuttle**, and **direct airline service is not available** from MKT currently. Do not count a ground shuttle as a scheduled flight into Mankato in the game.

### SDL — Scottsdale, Arizona: no commuter/scheduled airline, but GA/corporate services

[Scottsdale's official airport general information](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/scottsdale-airport/general-information) says **no commercial commuter or airline service** and identifies extensive corporate jet activity. Its [noise page](https://www.scottsdaleaz.gov/scottsdale-airport/airport-noise) describes voluntary nighttime noise abatement, not an absolute airport closure. Charter/air taxi and scheduled airline operations must be evaluated separately.

### CFO — Colorado Air and Space Port: identifier/history and separate spaceport permissions

The registry legacy `source_ident=KFTG`, while the currently researched airport uses `CFO/KCFO`. Preserve the original airport key and reconcile historical FAA aliases/coordinates before display changes. A spaceport operating license does **not** automatically authorize passenger or cargo airline flights. [Adams County airport profile](https://adamscountyco.gov/our-county/casp/about-casp/).

### Operational holds are specific, not airport-wide permanent bans

- **EMM Kemmerer**: high-elevation Wyoming small/medium business-aircraft planning role requires aircraft-specific runway performance and winter review; state planning label is not game tier. [Wyoming DOT](https://dot.state.wy.us/home/aeronautics/planning--programming-program/wyoming-statewide-system.html).
- **MHV Mojave** and **MZJ Pinal Airpark**: commercial aircraft storage, maintenance, teardown and spaceport activity cannot be converted into evidence of scheduled passenger/cargo service or unrestricted runway access. [Mojave operator](https://www.mojaveairport.com/contracts-leasing), [Pinal County](https://www.pinal.gov/1832/Services).
- **PNE Northeast Philadelphia**: separate main and secondary paved runways (approximately 7,000ft/5,000ft), urban controlled airspace and customs with prior arrangements require route/aircraft-specific screening. [Airport operator](https://www.phl.org/PNE/pilot-information).
- **SAA Shively Field**: wind shear/wildlife and high-altitude conditions warrant additional aircraft and owner checks, not automatic permanent ineligibility. [FBO](https://saratogajetcenter.com/).
- **FST Fort Stockton**: a runway closure NOTAM ending October 23 does **not** establish runway still closed on October 24. Recheck actual active notices. [FAA-derived NOTAM source](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KFST).
