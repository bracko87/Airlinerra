1. **Naples APF — future service, not active service.** Naples Airport Authority approved American/PSA on 16 October 2026 for twice-daily Charlotte flights **scheduled for 2 December 2026**. The CRJ700 proposal was under the airport's binding **75,000lb max takeoff weight**. The **22:00–07:00 curfew is voluntary** and not itself a runway closure. No assertion of actual scheduled flights having operated by 25 October 2026. Sources: https://www.flynaples.com/naples-airport-approves-operating-agreement-with-american-airlines/ and https://www.flynaples.com/faq/
2. **Polson 8S1 — aircraft and water runway distinct.** Lake County board lists **18/36 4195x75ft asphalt**, **3W/21W 4000x500ft water**, and fuel. Seaplanes/boats near the field require separate coordination, not automatic closure of the paved runway. Main runway is S-17 and nearby highway, ridge, ditch and mountain weather remain aircraft-specific.
3. **Avon Park AVO — mixed traffic not total closure.** City-owned airport supports corporate, training and military operations. A separate live NOTAM/traffic separation, owner approval and carrier-certification gate remains.
4. **Burns BNO — seasonal birds do not permanently close the runway.** FAA-derived 1 Oct 2026 record shows 5101ft concrete 12/30 with Jet A/avgas, while bird flocks are documented February–May and September–October. Separate RW03 is subject to a 600ft displaced threshold. This requires airframe- and runway-direction-specific checks.
5. **Boone BNW — concrete versus turf.** Iowa DOT confirms paved **15/33 4808x75ft** and turf **02/20 3248ft**. The turf runway fails Airlinerra pavement rules; main runway cannot be disqualified on that basis alone. RW15's 140ft threshold displacement, helicopter traffic and declared LDA remain to be verified.
6. **Hard aircraft limits retained:** Mignon Laird **4,000lb single-wheel** pavement; Townsend **5,000lb single-wheel**; Dayton Valley owner rule **under 12,500lb**. These are not overridden by public airport status or nominal runway length.
7. **Willard 8G1** has 300ft and 610ft displaced thresholds on nominal 4028ft runway; direction-specific actual declared LDA may fail 1200m. **Courtland 9A4** has poor concrete joints and markings and cannot progress without engineering inspection.
8. **Military/tribal/airspace rights:** Camp Guernsey military/civil access, Ak-Chin owner governance and Washington County correctional-facility overflight rule require actual permission or legal interpretation, not generic airfield eligibility.
9. Exactly **six** pre-agreed Airlinerra airport categories remain mandatory; **none assigned**. Airport must have operational asphalt or concrete meeting **1,200m direction-specific usable landing length**, adequate strength/width, airplane performance, FAA NOTAM, fuel/ground and legal owner/operator approval for an actual fleet.
