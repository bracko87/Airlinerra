## Priority evidence and distinctions

**M18 — Hope Municipal, Arkansas:** [Dated FAA-derived NOTAM](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/M18) announces **RW16/34 closure October 26 at 11:00Z through November 30 at 23:00Z, 2026**. That runway is **not yet closed by that notice on October 24**. The west taxiway has a longstanding closure, and remaining runway options need separate aircraft-specific assessment during the coming closure; do not mark the entire airport closed automatically.

**LXV — Lake County/Leadville, Colorado:** [Airport owner](https://www.lakecountyco.gov/285/Airport) and [October 2026 FAA-derived record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KLXV) confirm field elevation **9,934ft** and open asphalt primary **6,400×75ft**. Mountain density altitude, aircraft climb/payload, taxiway/ramp aggregate and potholes, and after-hours FBO permission require review. A long runway does not guarantee safe type-specific operations.

**XNX — Music City Executive, Tennessee:** [Official authority](https://www.musiccityexecutiveairport.com/airport-information/) confirms present airport code **XNX/KXNX**, historic registry **M33**, **6,300×100ft** runway, and that it is **not FAA Part 139 certificated**. The code change is an identity-reconciliation issue, and missing Part 139 does not automatically prohibit all lawful small charter services. Carrier type-specific certification remains to verify.

**LRJ — Le Mars Municipal, Iowa:** [Iowa DOT airport directory](https://secure.iowadot.gov/aviation/data_driven/publications/mapinfosys/AirportDirectory.aspx?FAACode=LRJ) reports **300ft displaced thresholds at both RW18 and RW36**. Separate physical runway length from official direction-specific LDA when checking aircraft fit.

**LNN — Lake County Executive, Ohio:** [Classic Jet Center](https://www.classicjetcenter.com/) advertises private charters and a named FAA carrier certificate number. This is positive charter evidence; active status, actual aircraft and airport owner permission remain unverified.

**LZU — Gwinnett County Briscoe, Georgia:** [County FAQ](https://www.gwinnettcounty.com/government/departments/transportation/aviation/faqs) confirms **no scheduled airline service**, but charter/business use through FBOs. Do not mark all commercial flying absent or attribute ATL flights here.

**LVM and LWC:** County and city sources respectively list **Yellowstone Air Service** and **Hetrick Air Services** as charter providers; actual legal certificate, fleet and airfield permissions remain pending.

**LVK — Livermore, California:** [City guidance](https://www.livermoreca.gov/departments/public-works/airport/airport-faqs) describes **voluntary** nighttime jet noise measures, not an absolute curfew.

**LWV — Lawrenceville–Vincennes, Illinois:** Two paved runways and a separate ultralight turf strip require runway-specific screening; turf is not a qualifying Airlinerra paved strip.
