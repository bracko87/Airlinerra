## Operating safeguards retained

1. Only a genuinely qualifying, operational **asphalt/concrete runway at least 1,200 metres** may be considered. A shorter, grass, failed or closed secondary runway cannot be substituted or conflated with a primary runway.
2. Validate real **direction-specific declared LDA**, aircraft runway width, pavement PCN/weight, terrain/obstacle and departure/climb profile, current and future-dated NOTAMs and wind/weather for the selected airplane. Never certify a flight on physical length alone.
3. Distinguish actual mandatory restrictions from voluntary training/noise guidance, ground-service hours, event-based prior permission, and scheduled construction. None grants automatic operator or commercial permissions.
4. Airport-owner authorization, services required for the actual route/aircraft, and legal airline/charter/cargo operator eligibility require separate verification.
5. No airport has been approved for gameplay, assigned any of Airlinerra's **exact six** classification categories, or activated in a world. The original 3,140-airport cohort remains unchanged.
6. Dates on older documents are not current FAA NOTAM clearances. Examples: Artesia's recorded RW22 PAPI outage was still time-effective on 25 October 2026; its stated expiry alone does not establish service restoration without a fresh source.
