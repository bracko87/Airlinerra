## Scope and verification standard

This is **operational eligibility triage** for 50 NEW expansion airports, after all **1,929/1,929 first-pass candidate source research reviews** were completed. Review of a published facility source **does not** confer operating rights. The assessment records specific runway restrictions, data conflicts and gaps in operator documentation; it does not invent airline permissions, aircraft fleet allocations, certified Part 121/135/139 eligibility, or gameplay admissions.

The Batch 05 records are stored in `airlinerra.airport_operational_triage`, separate from the immutable source registry and original 3,140-airport playable cohort. Most findings derive from documented earlier FAA-cycle source research; high-sensitivity cases (Naples, Ames, New Coalinga, Middleton, Cumberland, Vaiden, Wakefield) were additionally checked against accessible published airport/operator or FAA-derived listings on 2026-10-24. A stale or future-dated closure must be rechecked against live NOTAMs before any definitive operational decision.

## Significant findings to resolve first

### APF — Naples, FL: commercial service confirmed as future, not operational today

The Naples Airport Authority [approved the American Airlines operating agreement on October 16, 2026](https://www.flynaples.com/naples-airport-approves-operating-agreement-with-american-airlines/). Under the announced plan, PSA Airlines would operate twice daily to/from Charlotte using 65-seat CRJ-700s **starting December 2, 2026**. Airport rules state **maximum takeoff weight 75,000lb**, with a **voluntary 22:00–07:00 curfew**. As of **October 24**, the scheduled inaugural date remains in the future and must not be falsely counted as already operated. Stronger *commercial-service evidence* has emerged; **gameplay admission still requires its own review and approval**.

### AMW — Ames, IA: city reports primary runway PCI 45 and full reconstruction plan

Ames city's **May 26, 2026 staff report** describes Runway 01/19 as **PCI 45/100**, requiring full reconstruction rather than a routine overlay. Major **2027** construction/closures are *planned subject to grant awards*, not proven as currently active on October 24. Proposed multi-runway/phased closure must be rechecked with the city and current NOTAMs. [City report](https://vault.amesnews.net/gov/city/CouncilPackets/2026/060926CouncilAgenda/Runway_Staff_Report.pdf). Do not confuse planned 2027 closure with current Oct 2026 operating status.

### CBE — Greater Cumberland: actual WV airfield versus Maryland administration

The airport operator describes the actual airfield site as **Wiley Ford, Mineral County, West Virginia**, even though airport FAA administrative records and expansion dataset are associated with **Maryland**. [Owner's FAQ and address](https://cbeairport.com/). The operator explicitly confirms **no commercial airline flights**. Keep an accurate distinction between a physical state attribute and FAA administrative state; do **not** blindly recode or duplicate the airport. No route/game admission granted.

### A08 — Vaiden Field: first-pass prose refers to the wrong airport

The source reports **all airfield lights and beacon inoperable**, heavily faded runway markings and trees near RW16. The legacy source research note conflated a different airport. **Reconcile/correct prose and cross-check registry geolocation; do not mutate immutable source airport key without evidence.**

The main runway has markings/visual slope equipment issues that still require review. Do **not** characterize the entire airport as ineligible solely because the secondary runway is closed. Load/runway-by-runway screening required.
