## Airport-specific priority findings

**ACZ — Henderson Field / Wallace-Pender, North Carolina.** [NC Aviation official airport guide](https://www.ncdot.gov/divisions/aviation/NC%20Airports/AirportGuideACZ.pdf) and [FAA airport record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/ACZ) show paved RW09/27 **4,153×75ft** with **149ft/151ft displaced thresholds**, **12,500lb single-wheel** capacity and a rule restricting touch-and-go/practice approaches from **21:00–06:00**. Fuel and a pilot lounge are available. Public GA operation is not an automatic Part135/scheduled airline permit, and a practice restriction is not a blanket airport shutdown. Reconcile the historical/local Henderson Field versus modern Wallace-Pender naming under one immutable key.

**CHD — Chandler Municipal, Arizona.** [City of Chandler airport business directory](https://www.chandleraz.gov/business/chandler-municipal-airport/businesses-and-services) explicitly lists **Quantum Helicopters** for helicopter charter and flight training, plus several fixed-wing aviation businesses and full-service FBO fuel. The [city airport description](https://www.chandleraz.gov/business/chandler-municipal-airport/about) says there were no scheduled airlines as of its 2019 statement. Preserve active charter *leads* distinct from scheduled airline operations and confirm the specific carrier certificate and type of aircraft. Assess the multiple paved runways and busy Phoenix controlled airspace separately.

**COQ — Cloquet/Carlton County, Minnesota.** [FAA North Central Chart Supplement](https://aeronav.faa.gov/Upload_313-d/supplements/CS_NC_20250612.pdf) lists RW18/36 **4,002×75ft** with **S-8 / D-12** published pavement weight ratings (8,000lb single wheel, 12,000lb dual wheel). October 2026 records report REIL outages; confirm actual effective NOTAM and available alternate runway approach lighting before use. Outage of runway-end lights does not necessarily mean the entire airport is closed.

**AXA — Algona, Iowa.** The municipal runway dimensions differ between city and Iowa DOT publications and a displaced RW13 threshold is documented. Owner fuel availability does not resolve the FAA as-built length or aircraft-specific usable landing distance.

**BNW — Boone Municipal, Iowa.** Iowa DOT lists a **4,808ft** concrete primary with an approximately **140ft RW15 threshold displacement**, plus a shorter turf strip and military helicopter traffic. Do not use the turf strip as an equivalent paved commercial runway.

**C75 — Marshall County, Illinois.** Main RW pavement about **4,003ft**, separate crosswind runway **2,199ft** with different published load capability. FAA reports wildlife and nearby wind turbines. Verify each runway and current obstruction notices separately.

**CNC — Chariton Municipal, Iowa.** FAA-derived notices include runway REIL/VASI outages with published dates into **March 2027**, while the shorter secondary runway is below 1,200m. Check current effective NOTAM; approach-light failure is not automatic airport-wide closure.

**BFK — Buffalo Municipal, Oklahoma.** State aviation authority describes unattended 4,000×60ft public GA runway without listed fuel or ground transport and zero based jets in its 2023 profile. Physical paved length is not evidence of turbine handling or carrier licensing.

**BTP — Pittsburgh/Butler Regional, Pennsylvania.** Airport owner says **no scheduled commercial airlines**, yet reports private charter flights and medevac activity. Preserve positive on-demand aviation evidence rather than mislabel all commercial flying absent.

**BST — Belfast Municipal, Maine.** City calls the airport open 24/7 subject to current NOTAM and advises that winter runway conditions are normally reported during weekday hours. Winter access must be checked by date; do not infer permanent closure.

## No gameplay or real-world flight approval

All **1,929** expansion airports remain `NOT_APPROVED` and existing **3,140** game airports are unchanged. Airlinerra uses exactly six categories: **Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub**. Outside regulatory classifications or airport names do not assign those game tiers. All future airplane/operator use requires independent current FAA/owner confirmation of usable paved landing distance, aircraft wheel bearing, effective NOTAM, terrain/weather, ground support, commercial-use permission and relevant Part121/135/139 rules. This record is source-based game research, not authorization of commercial flight.
