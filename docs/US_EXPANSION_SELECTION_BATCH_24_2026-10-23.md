## Notable source-backed findings
- **DVN Davenport:** city says on-airport Jet Air Inc provides charter, air ambulance and air freight. Source: https://www.davenportiowa.com/services/public_works/airport
- **TNU Newton:** city FBO Johnson Aviation offers aircraft charter. Source: https://www.newtongov.org/189/Newton-Municipal-Airport
- **STF Starkville:** county explicitly says George M Bryan Field offers no commercial flights. Source: https://www.oktibbeha.ms.gov/183/George-M-Bryan-Airport
- **RUE Russellville:** city calls its airport a non-commercial airport. Source: https://russellvillear.gov/149/Russellville-Regional-Airport
- **GKT Gatlinburg-Pigeon Forge:** airport operator says no commercial aircraft flights, while local travel authority says private charter aircraft may land; do not equate a missing scheduled airline with a complete charter ban. Source: https://www.gktairport.com/
- **20V Kremmling:** Grand County confirms 7,415ft elevation, high terrain, and airport accommodates medium twins/light business jets; winter snow clearance and noise sensitive hours. Source: https://www.co.grand.co.us/1092/9851/Kremmling-Airport-20V
- **AJR Habersham County:** operator lists Air Methods air ambulance; airport-specific FAA record identifies a Taxiway A wing-span restriction for >80ft aircraft. Source: https://www.habershamga.com/airport.cfm and https://www.airnav.com/airport/AJR
- **4I3 corrected identity:** Knox County Airport at Mount Vernon OH is `4I3`; Morrow County at Mount Gilead OH is `4I9`. FAA/Ohio state confirms. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/4I3
- **PLD runway discrepancy resolved during Batch 24:** Portland IN city's website still describes a 4,002ft historical runway, but Oct 2026 FAA data lists runway 09/27 as **5,505 x 75 ft**, with low 12.5 single-wheel load rating. This **matches** existing Supabase ~1,677.9m, so no runway length change needed. Airport's case log includes dated external-source reconciliation. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/KPLD
- **CTJ West Georgia Regional:** FBO provides cargo handling and forklift service; this **does not prove airline cargo flights**. Source: https://wgraa.com/
