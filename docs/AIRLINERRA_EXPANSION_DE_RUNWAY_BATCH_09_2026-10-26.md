### Preserved distinction: physical runway vs. aerodrome operating state

The game’s present **airport collection** criterion is at least one **not-closed** paved runway with **physical gross runway length ≥1,200m**, asphalt or concrete. Independent runway geometry does **not** establish an operating permit for a particular commercial aircraft, and a short direction-specific **LDA** does not automatically mean the gross runway fails the physical collection rule.

For **EDCP** specifically, the newly identified **NOTAM declares the aerodrome closed during specified hours**. That *time-limited closure* is a reason for a **hold while in effect**, not permanent removal of a physically runway-qualified airport. The underlying restricted-airspace program through 2028 is likewise **not a continuous aerodrome-closure statement**. Before aircraft use, an official, fresh NOTAM and AIP check is mandatory.
