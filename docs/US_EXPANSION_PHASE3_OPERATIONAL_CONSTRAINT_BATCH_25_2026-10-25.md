## Research distinctions and admission safeguards

1. **Twin Lakes S17:** South Carolina's official Aeronautics Commission page (updated August 2026) states runway **06/24 4000x60ft asphalt** and **CLOSED TO AIRCRAFT WITH MAXIMUM GROSS WEIGHT 12,500LB AND ABOVE**. This is a binding runway/aircraft closure, **NOT a landing fee**. Transient fuel and parking require advance arrangement; a displaced RW06 approach and nonstandard lights add further checks. Source: https://aeronautics.sc.gov/airports/twin-lakes-airport
2. **Russell Municipal RSL:** Kansas DOT's 2025–26 airport directory states **17/35 5000x75ft concrete** and distinct **03/21 1602x300ft turf**, while Russell City describes the main concrete as **5500ft**. The turf runway fails Airlinerra paved-surface and length rules but does not close the separate paved main. The FAA geometry/source conflict must be resolved before actual LDA/fleet approval. Sources: https://aviation.ks.gov/files/KDOT_Airport_Directory_2025-2026.pdf and https://www.russellcity.org/155/Airport
3. **Tulsa Riverside RVS:** Airport-owner details distinguish asphalt runways **01L/19R 5101x100ft**, **01R/19L 4207x100ft** and **13/31 2641x50ft**. The third strip is below 1200m while the two longer ones can undergo independent landing-length and load checks. FAA reports complex Class D/underlying Class C training traffic and tower operating **07:00–22:00 local**. None establishes an airline license or gameplay approval. Sources: https://flytulsa.com/tulsa-river-side/airfield-details/ and https://www.faa.gov/flight_deck/rvs
4. **Mifflin County RVL:** FAA chart supplement describes **summer glider operations**, an operating paved 5001ft runway and after-hours Jet A only **by appointment**. Glider separation is an operational traffic matter rather than evidence of airport-wide closure; owner commercial permissions and a certified operator remain required. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/RVL
5. **Russellville Regional RUE:** Official city owner uses the term **noncommercial**. Do not overrule it based on crop-spraying/GA activity, and do not assume a ban extends to each Part 135 service without written owner clarification. Remains held.
6. **Rexburg RXE:** The RW17 displaced threshold and high-elevation performance must be verified using direction-specific LDA; gross paved length alone is not eligibility.
7. **Sunriver S21:** A private/resort public-use airfield may impose separate access and commercial charter terms, and displaced RW18 threshold/trees require precise aircraft landing checks. Remains held.
8. **Springer S42:** **~8500lb single-wheel pavement limit**, locked road gates, very high elevation and missing on-field fuel are genuine hard limits. Do not advance based on length alone.
9. **Warroad RRT:** An *International* airport title is not CBP flight permission. Verify country-specific legal customs arrangements and extreme winter support.
10. **Stanford S64, Lincoln S69 and Idaho County GIC:** Mountain/high-plains obstacle, winter and density-altitude constraints remain type- and dispatch-specific. Do not confuse Lincoln Montana S69 with Nebraska airline airport LNK.
