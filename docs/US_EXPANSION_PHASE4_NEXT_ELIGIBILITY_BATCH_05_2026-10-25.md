## Critical cross-airport distinctions

**C35 Reedsburg:** Runway 18/36 4,840ft gross but **published LDA only 4,100ft (1,250m) each direction**, with RW18 and RW36 displaced thresholds and date-dependent REIL conditions. Official LDA takes precedence over physical strip length; short runway 07/25 fails 1,200m. [FAA runway data](https://www.airnav.com/airport/C35).

**BST, C29, C75, CCY:** Primary paved lengths 4,000–4,003ft allow only ~19–20m gross margin above 1,200m, so directional landing distance and aircraft weight/width are vital. At Belfast, Dec–Mar runway condition reports are published daily 1400–2200Z, **not** evidence of safe dispatch outside those reporting hours. [BST](https://www.airnav.com/airport/BST) · [C29](https://www.airnav.com/airport/C29) · [C75](https://www.airnav.com/airport/C75) · [CCY](https://www.airnav.com/airport/CCY).

**C75 Marshall County:** The 4,000lb single-wheel loading applies to **short secondary 18/36**, not unverified capacity on main 13/31. 100LL and UL94 are listed; do not assume Jet A is available. [FAA source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/C75).

**BWP, C80, CAD:** Short turf or closed failed secondary runways cannot count toward Airlinerra's paved-runway rule; they also do not automatically close a separate operating paved primary. C80's primary has marking issues and RW30 glide-slope indicator trouble that still require active NOTAM verification. [BWP](https://www.airnav.com/airport/BWP) · [C80](https://www.airnav.com/airport/C80) · [CAD](https://www.airnav.com/airport/CAD).

**BXK Buckeye:** Skydiving drop zone east of the runway, prohibited overflight routes and low military jet traffic are real aircraft dispatch constraints, not a permanent physical runway closure. Jet A requires prior arrangement; city business listings are not automatic paid-flight rights. [FAA source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/BXK) · [City](https://www.buckeyeaz.gov/residents/buckeye-municipal-airport).
