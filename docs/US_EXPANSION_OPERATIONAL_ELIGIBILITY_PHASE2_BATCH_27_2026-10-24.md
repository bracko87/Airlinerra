**Methodology:** Airlinerra's minimum runway screen is a usable, operating paved asphalt/concrete runway at least 1,200m long. This first physical screen does not grant actual aircraft suitability or commercial flight authority. Each airport must separately pass actual direction-specific LDA, aircraft wheel/gear loading, weather/terrain, current FAA NOTAM and procedures, available fuel/staffing, owner's permission and operator air-carrier certificates. A `CONSTRAINT_CONFIRMED_HOLD` is not an airport-wide permanent rejection; a `EVIDENCE_GAP_HOLD` does not mean no charter ever operates. A code reconciliation is not permission to duplicate or mutate an immutable airport key.

## Critical source findings

### SOP — Moore County, North Carolina: actual restriction for air-carrier capacity

The [Moore County Airport owner pilot information](https://www.moorecountyairport.com/pilot-information/) and [FAA-derived aircraft remarks](https://uk.flightaware.com/resources/airport/KSOP/remarks) explicitly state airport is **closed to unscheduled air-carrier operations with more than 30 passenger seats**, and requires **12 hours' advance permission for jet flights 23:00–06:00**. Owner also requires avoidance of grass/turf surfaces by fixed-wing aircraft. **The main paved runway is open to public aviation**, so neither the >30-seat rule nor nighttime PPR implies a blanket airport closure or categorical ban on smaller licensed charters. Any proposed fleet must be screened against the actual rule and current notices.

### TIP — Rantoul National Aviation Center, Illinois: 48-hour aircraft-load PPR

The [October 2026 FAA-derived airport remarks](https://www.airnav.com/airport/TIP) give **48 hours' prior permission for aircraft with single-wheel load above 15,000lb**, note that many taxiways are **35ft wide**, and state the FBO is normally only staffed on weekdays unless advance weekend arrangements are made. The >15,000lb threshold is **PPR**, not proof all heavier aircraft are structurally forbidden. Confirm actual airport/operator permission and taxi width for the aircraft, and do not infer 24-hour support.

### SUA — Witham Field, Florida: voluntary nighttime noise policy

The [Martin County airport owner pilot information](https://www.martin.fl.us/pilot-information) and [county airport FAQs](https://www.martin.fl.us/martin-county-airport-witham-field-faqs) describe a **voluntary 23:00–07:00 noise curfew**; air ambulance, rescue and government operations are exempt. Tower hours are around **07:00–22:00**. A voluntary noise program is **not** a legally mandated blanket runway closure. The airport lists FBOs and charter services, but individual operator certificates, aircraft and flight schedules still require verification.

### CVB — Castroville Municipal, Texas: published aircraft capacity is not unlimited

The [City of Castroville](https://www.castrovilletx.gov/2338/Castroville-Municipal-Airport) describes a **B-II general-aviation airport**, a **15,000lb single-wheel class**, a description referring to **up to 12 passengers**, and a 5,001ft paved runway. This informs aircraft-specific compatibility and possible owner operating standards; it is not equivalent to certification of any scheduled airline. Obtain the official structural pavement rating and local permissions for each proposed commercial aircraft.

### T82 — Gillespie County, Texas: separate helicopter and private-jet charter leads

The [county's aviation business directory](https://www.gillespiecounty.gov/1327/Airport-Businesses) lists **Tango 82 Aviation** as **Part 135-certified helicopter charter** and also lists **Ladybird Jet** offering private charters and promoting scheduled semi-private service. These are distinct forms of positive commercial-service evidence and should not be misclassified as 'no charter'. The actual current operator certificate, operating aircraft, flight timetable and fixed-wing usage rights remain verification tasks. A helicopter operator certificate does not establish fixed-wing scheduled service.

### RNT — Renton Municipal, Washington: manufacturer flights and dated work

The [City of Renton airport](https://www.rentonwa.gov/Government/Departments-and-Offices/Public-Works/Airport) confirms Boeing 737 factory first flights alongside GA use, not 737 passenger-airline schedules. The [capital improvement updates](https://www.rentonwa.gov/Government/Departments-and-Offices/Public-Works/Airport/Capital-Improvement-Projects) say Phase 1 Taxiway Alpha works **completed September 2026**, October 13 runway mapping closure was past on the October 24 assessment date, and Phase 2 timing information is due in **early 2027**. Do not carry a past closure into all future game days.

### TXW — Mid Valley International, Texas: historic T65 to TXW and cargo maintenance distinction

The current [FAA instrument procedure record](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/flight_info/aeronav/procedures/application/index.cfm?event=procedure.results&nasrId=TXW) identifies **TXW/KTXW** at Weslaco, while older registry source code is **T65**. Reconcile under its immutable key. [South Texas Aviation Services](https://southtexasaviationservices.com/) advertises maintenance for Part 91/135 and cargo-airline aircraft, but a **maintenance contract is not an actual scheduled air-cargo flight** at TXW.

## Gameplay and operating safeguards

- All **1,929 expansion airports remain `NOT_APPROVED`**. No world activation, airport-category approval or edit to original **3,140** playable airports occurred.
- Airlinerra's exactly **six game categories** remain **Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub**. External aviation "Regional Business", "Executive", "International" and airframe class descriptions are **not** automatically in-game category assignments.
- A public airport may have real and current private charters, cargo handling, air ambulance or test flying while still lacking scheduled airline service. Preserve those distinctions in all future classifications.
- Aircraft operating compliance requires current FAA/NOTAM, airport owner/tribal permissions, pavement/load/declared LDA, terrain/winter/weather and verified air operator certification. Phase 2 is a research hold and **not a real-world flight-operating authorization**.
