## Key distinctions

**Olney Municipal ONY:** Current FAA-derived runway record distinguishes **RW04/22 5100x75ft good asphalt** from **RW13/31 5099x75ft failed condition**, with severe cracking, vegetation, stones and poor markings. RW17/35 also differs. Do not carry a FAILED 13/31 label to the entire airport, but continue to prohibit routine modeling of the unsafe 13/31 until corrected. Independently assess aircraft wheel load, directional LDA, Jet A and local paid-use permission. [FAA-derived Olney runway data](https://www.airnav.com/airport/ONY)

**Oxnard OXR:** The airport is physically operational, while an earlier FAA Part 139 listing reports inactive certification and Oxnard remarks require ARFF coordination for some commercial passenger aircraft. Under FAA rules, **scheduled passenger flights with more than nine seats and unscheduled flights with at least 31 passenger seats**, when covered by Part 139, require appropriate airport certification. Do not infer a physical closure from an administrative certificate status; equally, do not authorize Part139-covered airline service without valid certification and ARFF. [FAA Part139 applicability](https://www.faa.gov/airports/airport_safety/part139_cert/airports-affected)

**Starke County OXI:** Agricultural aerial application involves airfield traffic sequencing, not automatically a permanent runway closure. This advance does not verify FAA runway loading or commercial operator certification.

**Greater Portsmouth Regional PMH:** Skydiving and air tours likewise require event-specific NOTAM and parachute coordination but are not themselves evidence of permanent closure or a licensed passenger/cargo carrier. [Scioto County airport](https://www.sciotocountyoh.com/airport)

**Orange County ORG, Texas:** The owner explicitly does not accommodate commercial airline traffic; this genuine owner prohibition remains a hold until the county clarifies whether any narrower certificated on-demand charter/cargo flights are permissible. Never infer permission from corporate GA movements.

**Polacca P10, Arizona:** Serious pavement cracking, rocks/vegetation and a road crossing the 50ft-wide paved runway create hard safety and owner-access problems, not a mere documentation issue.

**Stellar Airpark P19:** Residential/private owner consent, advance permission for transient aircraft, gross-weight limits and displaced thresholds require verified operating rights.

**Cottonwood P52:** FAA-derived runway strength ~4000lb single-wheel is a severe physical constraint until engineering evidence validates a higher rating. 100LL alone does not establish commercial turbine fueling.

**Palm Beach County Glades PHK:** Runway 18/36 has displaced thresholds and published declared landing distances; the physical 1255m runway length cannot replace directional usable LDA at least 1200m.

**Central Maine/Norridgewock OWK:** A weather station installation contract or award does not demonstrate that AWOS was installed, commissioned or is operational for a commercial dispatch. Maintain the hold pending actual weather service and winter runway maintenance.
