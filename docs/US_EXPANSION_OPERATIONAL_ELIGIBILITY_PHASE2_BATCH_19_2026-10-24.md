## Scope and safeguards

All **1,929** U.S. expansion candidates already received first-pass, source-backed FAA/airport research in Phase 1. Phase 2 separately records **runway physical/declared distance and weight**, facility limitations, temporary operating restrictions, charter/cargo operator leads, old identifier inconsistencies and explicitly unresolved permissions. A constraint hold is **not automatically a permanent whole-airport rejection**, and an evidence-gap hold is **not proof commercial charter service is illegal or absent**. FAA/airport-owner conditions and commercial operator certification must be independently validated before any flight decision or Airlinerra gameplay approval.

Each Batch 19 row documents the original immutable airport key, the specific issue, finding, action required, one or more source URLs, effective reference date and checked timestamp. First-pass source research remains unmodified. Temporary NOTAM and facility conditions may expire or be repaired.

## Priority evidence — operating rules and commercial-service distinctions

### GNG Gooding (Idaho) — runway length conflicts across owners and FAA

The [City of Gooding's airport page](https://www.goodingidaho.org/pages/airport) says its reconstructed runway is **4,135ft**, whereas an [FAA 2025–26 Chart Supplement](https://aeronav.faa.gov/Upload_313-d/supplements/CS_NW_20250220.pdf) and an [FAA 2026 RNAV approach chart](https://aeronav.faa.gov/d-tpp/2601/00657R25.PDF) identify runway 07/25 at **5,371×75ft**, matching the NASR candidate's approximate 1,637m runway. The municipal page's length should not silently overwrite more recent aviation publications; get current manager/FAA as-built declared distances and reconcile the discrepancy before admitting aircraft. FAA source also flags seasonal cropdusting and helicopter traffic. Neither source discrepancy alone proves runway closure.

### GMU Greenville Downtown (South Carolina) — affirmative charter providers

The [airport's current operator services listing](https://www.greenvilledowntownairport.com/services) names **DoubleDay Aviation** and **Special Services Corporation** as air charter/air taxi providers. [Special Services Corporation](https://www.flyssc.com/charters/) advertises on-demand aircraft departing GMU, including King Air and Citation types. This is affirmative commercial charter evidence—**not** an automatic scheduled passenger airline route, permission for every aircraft or Airlinerra approval. Validate specific current operator certificates and individual aircraft.

### FMY Page Field (Florida) — charter operations but no scheduled airline since 1983

The [Lee County Port Authority's airport history](https://www.flylcpa.com/fmy/about-page-field/) confirms Page Field switched to general aviation in **1983** and has had **no scheduled passenger airlines since then**. Its [current airport homepage](https://www.flylcpa.com/fmy/) acknowledges business, air charter, medical and flight-training activity. Nearby scheduled service at RSW must never be attributed to FMY. The recorded evidence-gap hold concerns specific certified charter fleets and game admission, **not** the existence of all charter flights.

### GKT Gatlinburg–Pigeon Forge (Tennessee) — VFR/approach limitation and charter distinction

[Airport operator](https://www.gktairport.com/) reports **no instrument approaches** currently and states no commercial airline flights; the [Gatlinburg tourism agency](https://www.gatlinburg.com/plan/frequently-asked-questions/) explicitly notes **private charters can fly into GKT**, while TYS is the nearby commercial airline airport. This does not prove all private charters are banned. A [May 2026 runway relocation project completion report](https://www.gmcnetwork.com/news/2026/05/gatlinburg-pigeon-forge-airport-celebrates-grand-opening-after-runway-relocation/) further makes older runway data worth rechecking. Require current instrument and runway conditions and actual charter/operator permission before approving a game fleet.

### FWS Fort Worth Spinks (Texas) — owner's large-aircraft controls

Spinks is **not** Dallas/Fort Worth International (DFW); retained [Fort Worth city airport information](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/aviation/spinks/general-aviation) describes GA/business/charter service and indicates larger-aircraft operations require owner-specific review. The earlier source note mentions **Boeing 737 prior permission**; treat its precise current applicability as **a verification task**, not unquestioned authorization or a blanket physical runway ban. Airfield pavement, apron, airplane type, passenger seats and operator permit must be checked with the owner.

### FXE Fort Lauderdale Executive (Florida) — charter companies, not scheduled airlines

[City-operated FXE charter directory](https://www.flyfxe.com/visitors/charters) lists private charter services and separately says the field does **not** offer scheduled passenger airline flights. Do not transfer Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL) routes to FXE.

### FFZ, FLP, FMM, EPH — operating traffic hazards vs airport eligibility

- **FFZ Mesa Falcon Field:** [FAA risk guide](https://www.faa.gov/flight_deck/ffz) warns of parallel runway wrong-surface confusion and dense VFR training traffic. Special flight procedures and aircraft-specific ground/ramp access are distinct from a whole-airport closure.
- **FLP Marion County Regional:** [Arkansas airport directory](https://fly.arkansas.gov/uploads/7/5/7/7/75772239/flippin.pdf) lists ultralight flight activity near the field and after-hours fuel fees; note traffic separation and scheduled handling.
- **FMM Fort Morgan:** [City airport description](https://www.cityoffortmorgan.com/80/Airport) describes agricultural flights and corporate aviation with staffing and weather-dependent hours. Check spray-traffic windows and fuel; do not infer a named airline.
- **GPZ Grand Rapids/Itasca:** Earlier FAA-derived research reports a VOR/DME outage and taxi marking concerns; as with every navigation NOTAM, verify effective dates before relying on the outage.

### FDK from Batch 18, not counted in Batch 19

Frederick Municipal Maryland was completed in Batch 18. Its **landing fee threshold of 12,500lb for non-based aircraft** is not a runway structural weight ceiling. This cross-check is contextual only; FDK was not assessed again in Batch 19.
