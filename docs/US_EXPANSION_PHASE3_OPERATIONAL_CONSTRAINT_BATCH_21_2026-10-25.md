## Important distinctions and protected eligibility rules

1. **Lompoc LPC:** the City of Lompoc publishes a **4600ft x 100ft asphalt runway 07/25**, self-service 24-hour 100LL/Jet A and on-field skydiving. The skydiving program needs separation, dated notices and owner-authorized slots; it does **not** by itself close the entire runway or establish permitted commercial air service. City source: https://www.cityoflompoc.com/government/departments/public-works/transportation/lompoc-airport
2. **Lampasas LZZ:** the city describes a **current 4200ft runway extendable to 5000ft**. Airport advisory committee minutes discuss proposed alternative runway improvements and a 5000ft extension. **Do not treat the planned 5000ft as built pavement, nor treat a proposed construction project as an existing runway closure** absent a relevant actual NOTAM. City source: https://www.lampasas.org/117/Airport and 2025 meeting minutes https://www.lampasas.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_01082025-523
3. **Midland Airpark MDD:** Midland's official aviation authority applies landing fees when certified maximum gross landing weight **exceeds 30,000lb**; a fee is **not a pavement weight limit**. Two distinct asphalt runways are **07/25 5022x75ft** and **16/34 3977x75ft**, so the former's aircraft suitability cannot automatically transfer to the latter. Do not import scheduled airline activity from Midland International Airport (MAF). Official source: https://www.flymaf.com/airport-information/non-tenant-landing-fees.html and https://www.flymaf.com/airport-information/airpark/
4. **Marion Municipal MNN:** the FAA Oct 2026 airport remarks impose a **35ft wingspan maximum on Taxiway D between runway and ramp**, NOT an entire airport or runway wingspan restriction. Wider-aircraft compatibility still requires actual approved alternate taxi route, apron and taxi clearance. Nearby gliders/wildlife and after-hours Jet A by prior coordination remain open. Source: https://airnav.com/airport/MNN
5. **Mount Pleasant MPZ:** current Iowa DOT source confirms **15/33 concrete 4001x75ft**, versus independent **03/21 turf 1967ft**. The turf secondary is ineligible under Airlinerra's paved-runway-only rules but does not disqualify the qualifying-length concrete primary; pavement limits and landing distances still require type-specific approval. The city advertises charter services but a specific current operator certificate and applicable carrier permissions must still be validated. Sources: https://secure.iowadot.gov/aviation/data_driven/publications/mapinfosys/AirportDirectory.aspx?FAACode=MPZ and https://cityofmountpleasantiowa.org/departments/public-works-utilities/airport/
6. **Frank Federer Memorial M36:** each landing direction has a displaced threshold on an otherwise near-4000ft runway, so directional usable LDA can fall below 1200m despite a physically qualifying paved strip; a published 12000lb single-wheel rating also constrains aircraft.
7. **Alpine County M45:** high-elevation winter terrain and FAA-reported **POOR** pavement constitute real holds; a long-enough runway cannot bypass pavement safety.
8. **Larned/Pawnee County LQR:** an owner web page citing **2013 FAA information** must not be mistaken for a current 2026 runway and air-service authorization. Fuel availability must be rechecked.
9. **Ellington LUG and Mission Field LVM:** owner-reported existing charter/fuel capacity is useful but does not independently prove chosen fleet approval, runway aircraft strength, current licensed Part 135 activity or reliable weather dispatch. Preserve each pending aviation gate.
10. Exactly **six pre-agreed airport categories** are used in Airlinerra. None were assigned here. An airport cannot enter gameplay without an **operational asphalt/concrete runway with a suitable 1200m usable directional LDA**, width/strength, airplane performance, current dated NOTAM, actual fuel/emergency handling, airport-owner commercial permission and a properly certified commercial operator.
