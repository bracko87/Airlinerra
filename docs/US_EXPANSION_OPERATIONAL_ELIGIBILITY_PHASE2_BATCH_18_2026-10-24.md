## Scope, interpretation and source validity

Phase 1 first-pass research covers all **1,929** U.S. expansion candidates. Phase 2 is a **separate airport-operating-constraints and permission-evidence triage**, not FAA approval, a game-airport admission, permanent rejection or flight operational clearance. Prior independently researched FAA/airport evidence was individually interpreted with airport-specific follow-ups; high-impact constraints were checked against current published FAA-derived/owner sources. A general aviation FBO, Part135 charter base, aircraft tour, scheduled airline or freight support each represent different levels of evidence; they are not interchangeable. Time-sensitive conditions require current NOTAM confirmation, especially after a stated expiry date.

A runway closure must not be inferred from a **closed ramp, secondary runway or taxiway**. A fee threshold is not a physical aircraft weight restriction. Paved physical runway length, usable landing distance and aircraft performance must be separately evaluated. Positive commercial flight activity does not automatically authorize an unreviewed fleet or game admission.

## Critical and positive airport-specific findings

### E24 — Whiteriver, Arizona: White Mountain Apache tribal field with partial infrastructure restrictions

[October 2026 FAA-derived listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/E24) and [September–October 2026 Chart Supplement record](https://tallyaero.com/atlas/chart-supplement/SW_97/) show **RW01/19 6,350×75ft** at **5,153ft field elevation**, high canyon wind/terrain/wildlife, federal fire suppression traffic in spring through October, **east-side ramp closed**, multiple runway lights inoperative and RW01 PAPI/REIL and RW19 REIL unavailable indefinitely in the then-effective publication. Runway 19 has a **250ft displaced threshold**; published LDA for each direction is around **6,100ft**. There is **no basis from these remarks to declare the entire runway closed**. The airport is tribal-owned; official owner commercial permission and current FAA lighting/NOTAM plus available ramp access are independent gates.

### E41 — Reagan County, Texas: narrow primary, displacement and loose stone risk

[Current FAA-derived E41 runway listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/E41) says RW16/34 is **5,035×50ft asphalt**, rated **14,000lb single wheel**, with loose stones and a **1,005ft RW16 displaced threshold**. Simple subtraction gives about **4,030ft (1,228m)** for RW16 landing, only slightly exceeding the 1,200m usable-landing benchmark and requiring confirmation of official declared LDA and aircraft fit. RW09/27 is about **4,001×60ft paved POOR** with seal spalling. The poor secondary doesn't automatically close the primary or the airport, and neither runway grants commercial carrier permission on length alone.

### EPH — Ephrata, Washington: glider operations coexist with powered aircraft

[Seattle Glider Council's published operations guidance](https://www.seattleglidercouncil.org/ephrata) describes dedicated glider patterns on **RW04/22 alongside main powered runway RW03/21**, significant seasonal soaring, and an aerobatic box with applicable NOTAMs. Washington DOT's [airport profile](https://wsdot.wa.gov/travel/aviation/airports-list/ephrata-municipal) also identifies air ambulance and firefighting activity. This is genuine mixed-flight traffic; verify separation and special-use NOTAMs, not a claim that the public main runway is permanently closed.

### FDK — Frederick Municipal, Maryland: commercial charter exists; landing fee is not aircraft maximum

The [City of Frederick's July 1, 2026 fee schedule](https://cityoffrederick.com/1265/Airport-Services) confirms jet/turboprop charters and imposes landing fees for **non-based aircraft from 12,500lb**, with **waiver for based aircraft**. Fee tiers: $50 for 12,500–20,000lb, $100 for 20,001–50,000lb, and $200 above 50,001lb. This is a financial rule, **not a 12,500lb structural runway limit**. Separate actual runway load, airport access and named current air-carrier/charter certificates remain to verify.

### EKM — Elkhart, Indiana: active charter service, historic passenger schedule ended

[City of Elkhart airport services](https://www.cityofelkhartin.gov/departments/elkhart-municipal-airport-ekm/) expressly lists aircraft charter service, full aviation services and rescue/fire capability. Its [official airport FAQ](https://www.cityofelkhartin.gov/departments/elkhart-municipal-airport-ekm/municipal-airport-faq/) confirms scheduled passenger airline service **ended in 1987**. Preserve the positive charter evidence without falsely reviving historical scheduled airline routes. Active aircraft operator certificates and approved game fleet remain to be reviewed.

### MNE / RCE — historical FAA airport identifier reconciliation, not new airports

[Minden Airport's city source](https://mindenla.org/business/minden-airport/) and [current FAA-derived listing](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KMNE) use **MNE/KMNE** for its publicly operating Louisiana airfield, whereas the candidate registry stores **KF24** as a historical source identifier.
