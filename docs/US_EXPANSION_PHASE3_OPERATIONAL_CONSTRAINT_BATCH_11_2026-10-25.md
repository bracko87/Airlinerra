## Engineering and airfield-policy safeguards

1. **Only operational asphalt or concrete runways ≥1,200m** are candidates. Each selected aircraft must separately satisfy the **directional declared landing distance**, strength (PCN / individual wheel loading), width, takeoff/landing performance, terrain/obstacles, weather and current NOTAM.
2. **Rusk County (RCX):** The 4,001ft FAIR asphalt 14/32 primary is distinct from a 3,199ft POOR runway 01/19 with cracked and vegetated pavement. The latter does not authorize any flight or disqualify the primary by itself. Main-runway usable length, weight, trees and fuel remain to be checked.
3. **Sac City (SKI):** Iowa DOT's two separate concrete runways are 4,100ft 18/36 and 2,350ft 14/32. FAA documentation also flags mutual runway sightline limitations and 100LL-only fuel. The short runway is excluded; safe operation is not yet approved.
4. **Whiteside County (SQI), Toccoa (TOC) and Valentine/Miller Field (VTN):** Each has a qualifying nominal-length main paved runway physically distinct from a short secondary/crosswind. Thresholds, approach trees, pavement conditions and permissions must be treated independently.
5. **Cottonwood / H.A. Clark Memorial (CMR):** Official city directory explicitly says **Jet A is unavailable**, though 100LL is self-service around the clock. It remains held for turbine-fuel suitability, not incorrectly advanced based only on business/air-tour activity.
6. **Johnson County (6A4):** FAA-derived October data document both RNAV directions and Jet A, but PAPI obstacle clearance limitations and local trees/mountain peaks; source also reports live obstruction-light outages. This is a material aircraft-specific hold.
7. **Wayne County Kentucky (EKQ):** Official airport website states 24/7 access with a code and self-service fuel but limited weekday FBO staffing, whereas FAA effective Oct1 lists Monday–Saturday staffed and Sunday unattended. The operations distinction is not a closure, but the conflicting response-time/staff records prevent assuming scheduled passenger or cargo turnaround.
8. **Airlinerra's exact six airport categories** remain mandatory. This batch does not allocate a category, add an airport to the original 3,140, approve a new airport in any game world, or waive the final operating gate.
9. Every batch record carries `game_admission_status = NOT_APPROVED`. Source documents are neither legally binding owner approval nor official type-certificated carrier permissions.
