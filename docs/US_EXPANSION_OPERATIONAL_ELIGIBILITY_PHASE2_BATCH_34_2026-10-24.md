## High-priority Batch 34 findings

**CQB — Chandler Regional, Oklahoma.** [Oklahoma airport authority](https://oklahoma.gov/aerospace/airports/find-an-airport/chandler-regional.html) documents a **4,000 × 60ft** paved runway, avgas/Jet A and state 'Regional Business' classification, not an airline. Its [2026 capital program](https://oklahoma.gov/aerospace/outreach-advocacy/newsroom/oklahoma_greenlights_520_million_acp.html) calls for rehabilitation/extension to **5,000 × 75ft beginning in 2027**. Future dimensions must not be used for current aircraft eligibility. State planning additionally identifies a current approximately **12,500lb single-wheel** rating; independently check present weight classification before assigning an aircraft.

**E51 — Bagdad, Arizona.** The [Yavapai County airport operator](https://www.yavapaiaz.gov/Resident-Services/Public-Works/Airports-Division/Bagdad-Airport) says the 4,550 × 60ft runway is rated **4,000lb single wheel**, matching [FAA-derived September 2026 data](https://www.globalair.com/airport/apt.runway.aspx?aptcode=e51). But the [older 2014 master plan](https://apps.azdot.gov/files/Airports/MP_PDF/Bagdad/Bagdad_MP.pdf) says **12,000lb** and documents displaced thresholds. This source conflict is a **structural load and declared-distance reconciliation hold**, not permission to apply the higher historical rating. Airport sits at ~4,183ft elevation, also requiring hot/high airplane performance.

**EHA — Elkhart–Morton County, Kansas.** [Published NOTAMs](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KEHA) specify **all airport fuel unavailable until November 21, 2026**, all four PAPIs unusable through **December 31, 2026**, and RNAV (GPS) RW35 LPV / LNAV-VNAV minima unavailable. Check active FAA notices on any actual flight date and alternate fuel/approaches; these restrictions do not by themselves mean runway closure.

**F44 — Athens Municipal, Texas.** [Current FAA-derived airport description](https://www.airnav.com/airport/F44) shows a **3,988 × 60ft** paved runway (1,216m long, only 16m over the 1,200m threshold), with **25,000lb single-wheel** weight classification, nearby trees/road and wildlife. Jet A, 100LL and maintenance are positive amenities but not a certified airline. Review direction-specific LDA, obstacles, width and legal charter/cargo operator.

**GZL — Stigler Regional, Oklahoma.** Current airport is **GZL/KGZL** according to a [2020 city airport document](https://www.stiglerok.net/_files/ugd/5a4613_86042d41d15d436b8797360fdeaf35b8.pdf), whereas candidate registry source-ident is old **KF84**. Reconcile the historical FAA alias and physical coordinates without mutating its immutable airport key or adding duplicate airports. The word 'Regional' does not determine the game airport category.

**DKR — Houston County, Texas.** Published **S-12** (12,000lb single-wheel) runway limit and both PAPI units out indefinitely in retained 2026 source. Verify effective NOTAM and aircraft/approach capability; a PAPI outage is not an automatic runway shutdown.

**EYE — Eagle Creek Airpark, Indiana.** A [dated airport fuel notice](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KEYE) says 100LL self-service unavailable into December 2026; do not assume all other fuel or all runways closed. Do not transfer Indianapolis International's scheduled flights to EYE.

**CRG — Jacksonville Executive/Craig, Florida.** FDOT distinguishes visual-only RW05/23 from instrument-capable RW14/32; runway LDA, procedures and aircraft weight must be evaluated per runway, not only from the nominal maximum field length.

**EST — Estherville, Iowa.** Winter-closed turf runway is separate from its 4,797ft paved concrete primary. A seasonal turf restriction does not close the airport's operating paved runway.

**EPM — Eastport, Maine.** An airport-of-entry designation near Canada is not blanket permission to operate international commercial passenger or cargo flights; verify CBP notice and landing permissions and winter services.
