This **Phase 2** is a separate source-backed operational eligibility triage: runway dimensions/strength, terrain/seasonality, owner operating restrictions, known carrier services, missing operator proof, and source identity/code reconciliation. ## Evidence-led findings requiring priority follow-up

### 1G4 — Grand Canyon West, Arizona: published scheduled tourism flights

[Grand Canyon Scenic Airlines](https://www.scenic.com/flights/grand-canyon) explicitly lists **scheduled flights between Boulder City (BLD/BVU) and Grand Canyon West (1G4/GCW)**, with sample departures in each direction and a flight selector for travel dates. This is *positive evidence of a tourism air-service product*, not a claim the airport has zero commercial flights. It does not by itself confirm the operating status of a particular departure today, the precise aircraft/operator certificate, Hualapai/airport access rules or conventional scheduled-airline fleet eligibility. Keep `EVIDENCE_GAP_HOLD` until these specific questions and game admission are reviewed.

### 1R8 — Bay Minette Municipal, Alabama: live fuel restrictions are temporary

[Published current NOTAM listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/1R8) reports separate **Jet A and avgas unavailable through October 26, 2026**, with further self-service avgas limitations, and multiple nearby unserviceable obstacle-tower lights. The first-pass record also flagged restricted night operating conditions, but a live notice is required to establish its active status. Do **not** conclude the whole airport is permanently closed or carry the temporary fuel outage forward indefinitely. This is an operational **hold** with an explicit check at expiry.

### 20V — Kremmling/McElroy, Colorado: altitude and seasonal runway availability

[Grand County airport operator](https://www.co.grand.co.us/1092/9851/Kremmling-Airport-20V) specifies airport elevation **7,415ft**, runway **5,536×75ft**, and aircraft accommodation through **ARC B-II (medium twin turboprops/light business jets)**. The owner documents regular **November–April runway snow-removal work** during which a runway may close temporarily. That is *not a blanket winter airport closure*. It requires type-specific hot/high and runway-load checks plus current NOTAM before any game route.

### 2M2 — Lawrenceburg–Lawrence County, Tennessee: explicit pavement strengths

[City airport page](https://www.lawrenceburgtn.gov/222/Lawrenceburg-Lawrence-County-Airport) publishes **5,003×100ft runway**, **25,000lb single-wheel** and **37,000lb dual-wheel** maximum runway loading, with Jet A/avgas and service to business customers. Aircraft-specific wheel loads and certified carrier access require validation. The existence of business customers is positive aviation evidence, but not blanket passenger airline authorization.

### APS — Port of South Louisiana Executive Regional: cargo is a future goal

[Port operator service statement](https://portsl.com/airport-services/) confirms actual **corporate/charter jet support** with a **5,150ft lit runway**, fuel and 24h operations. It explicitly describes **future addition of cargo services** as a goal, not proof of an existing scheduled cargo airline. The airport registry code K1L0 versus contemporary APS/KAPS also requires alias lineage validation.

### Other significant airport constraints and identity cases

- **1U7 Bear Lake County, Idaho:** ~5,933ft elevation, source notes **no snow removal**, only 100LL and no repairs. Require winter airport status and aircraft-specific assessment.
- **33V Walden, Colorado / 38U Wayne Wonderland, Utah:** high-elevation paved airports with limited or unreported turbine fuel/ground support; aircraft loading/seasonal operation must be reviewed.
- **3T5 Fayette Regional Air Center, Texas:** [FAA-derived runway record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/3T5) lists **5,000×75ft asphalt, 30,000lb single-wheel**; [FBO information](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/3T5) confirms Jet A/avgas and prior-notice after-hours services. Current carrier certification is separate.
- **DWA Yolo County, California:** source-reported **fenced/secured aviation areas** mean non-tenant commercial access requires explicit airport/FBO coordination.
- **ORK / 2G9:** secondary paved or turf runways must be screened individually; a nonqualifying turf crosswind strip does not automatically close or exclude the primary paved runway.
- **JCA, BCR, APS, EBA, LUV, JHN, RYA, CPP and CNI:** recorded historical identifier changes, such as **19A→JCA**, **1J0→BCR** and **47A→CNI**, are flagged for **FAA alias/coordinate reconciliation**, not duplicate airport creation. Exactly one immutable key per physical airport.
- **15J, FFX, MQS and 1B1:** FBO or airport marketing of business aviation or charter facilities is not, on its own, a validated specific Part 135/121 operator/aircraft/schedule.
