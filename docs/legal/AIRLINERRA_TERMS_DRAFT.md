## Service and account
Airlinerra is an online fictional airline-management game. Players create an account, confirm their email, and create an airline in a designated game world. Managers must protect their credentials, may not impersonate others or interfere with game infrastructure, and should report account compromise.

## Game economy
Every new airline is initially allocated **$5,000,000 of in-game currency** and **zero aircraft**. These are **fictional in-game values, not cash, property, redeemable credits or payment balances**. Time advances at one in-game day per real day. Aircraft acquisition, route schedules, competition and virtual markets may evolve subject to published game rules.
