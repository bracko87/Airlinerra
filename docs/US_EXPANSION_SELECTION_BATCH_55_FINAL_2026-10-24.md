### Admission controls

**36/36 remain `NOT_APPROVED`**, as do all 1,929 source-reviewed expansion candidates. No airport was admitted, activated or classified for gameplay in this batch. Airlinerra supports exactly **six** airport gameplay classes: Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub. FAA classes and airport proper names do not automatically map to these categories.

**Recommended next step:** Move from first-pass source collection to a separate evidence-based admission triage: (1) exclude/hold closed or severe-condition paved runways; (2) resolve airport identity/geography and current permissions; (3) verify current pavement, declared runway lengths and each playable aircraft type; (4) verify commercial-use eligibility; (5) only then assign one of the six categories and request controlled gameplay admission. This keeps the original 3,140 playable airports unchanged until decisions are reviewed.
