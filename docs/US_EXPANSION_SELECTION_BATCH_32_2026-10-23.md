## High-impact source findings
- **T82 Gillespie County, TX**: Gillespie County explicitly lists **Tango 82 Aviation as Part 135-certified helicopter charter**, not a fixed-wing scheduled airline. Its FBO offers 24/7 100LL/Jet A; airport handles private/business aviation. Sources: https://www.gillespiecounty.gov/1327/Airport-Businesses ; https://www.gillespiecounty.gov/1217/Airport
- **MKS Berkeley County, SC**: state pilots' directory documents 5,001 × 75ft concrete runway and **650ft displaced threshold RWY05**, materially reducing usable landing distance for this direction. https://aeronautics.sc.gov/airports/berkeley-county-airport
- **ADG Lenawee County, MI**: county describes 5,000ft paved business aviation runway, separate **turf 11/29 closed December–April or when snow covered**, and 24h self-serve 100LL versus staffed Jet A. https://www.lenawee.mi.us/563/Pilot-Info
- **0R7 The Red River, LA**: current FAA remarks identify unattended public field, **beacon plus PAPI/REIL outages on both ends**, tree obstructions and ramp-use owner coordination. Runway remains technically 5,002ft paved; lighting requires review. https://airnav.com/airport/0R7
- **GDA Marion-Crittenden County KY**: FAA code **GDA**, formerly **5M9**. Do not duplicate the airport because of its previous identifier. https://www.airnav.com/airport/GDA
- **JER Jerome County, ID**: Precision Aviation advertises corporate-jet FBO with de-icing and airport services; charter contact exists, but a current air-carrier certificate is not established by FBO services alone. https://paviation.com/fbo.html
- **ORE Orange Municipal, MA**: airport-specific directory explicitly lists **charter** among services, alongside GA and parachute jumping. Current operator certification unresolved. https://www.fltplan.com/AirportInformation/ORE.htm
- **LPR Lorain County OH**: county expressly labels it a **general-aviation reliever airport**, with ILS runway and MRK Aviation FBO; no scheduled airline established. https://loraincountyohio.gov/313/County-Regional-Airport
- **TQK Scott City, KS**: municipal owner confirms military, **medical evacuation**, agricultural, corporate/private flying and on-field FBO, but no scheduled passenger or named commercial charter air carrier established. https://www.scottcityks.org/government/airport
- **BMQ Burnet TX**: owner confirms private/business aviation and **emergency medical** flying; these activities do not prove scheduled passenger service. https://www.cityofburnet.com/airport
- **DEQ Sevier County AR**: on-field airport source reports unattended with Jet A and 100LL 24h self-service, runway-end trees and local NOTAM/PPR guidance. https://seviercountyairport.com/
- **OXI Starke County IN**: airport FAA master record and state list establish public use, 100LL, agricultural activity, without a named charter service. https://www.airportiq5010.com/5010ReportRouter/default.aspx?airportID=OXI
