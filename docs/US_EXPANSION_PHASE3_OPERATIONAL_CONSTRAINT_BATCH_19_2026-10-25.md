## Important eligibility safeguards

1. **Grimes Field I74:** the FAA October 2026 airport record imposes landing fees on aircraft with maximum landing weight at least **6,000lb**, with a minimum fuel purchase waiver. This is a **fee, not a runway weight limit**. FAA-reported 02/20 4,400ft asphalt is distinct from a short grass strip. Source: https://www.airnav.com/airport/I74
2. **Hawkins Field HKS:** the Jackson Municipal Airport Authority confirms GA/business air traffic, Mississippi Army National Guard helicopter operations, PHI Air Medical, Pafford and FBO fueling. These tenants require military/medical priority coordination but do **not** by themselves close the public paved runway. Delta's flights there in 1929 are history, not today's airline service. Source: https://jmaa.com/hawkins-field/
3. **Natchitoches Regional IER:** city operator confirms main **17/35 5,000x150ft paved** and crosswind **07/25 4,000x100ft paved**, both lit, with 24-hour automated Jet A/100LL. Each direction, length, strength and airframe must be checked separately. Neither fleet permission nor current Part 121/135 rights follow from corporate-jet suitability. Source: https://www.natchitochesla.gov/government/city-departments/airport/
4. **Lake City Gateway LCQ:** city lists main **10/28 8,003x150ft** asphalt with **75,000lb single-wheel** pavement strength, versus **05/23 4,001x75ft** asphalt with much lower load class. FAA-derived 2026 data reports runway 10/28 **FAIR** even though older owner page describes it GOOD. Never collapse those into one runway condition or inherit main runway loads for 05/23. Source: https://www.lcfla.com/airport/page/gateway-airport
5. **South Texas Regional HDO:** four separate public runways have distinct runway dimensions; an airport name, tower, fuel and the longest runway cannot transfer full landing performance to the other three. Stay held until actual flight/runway-specific proof is available.
6. **Houlton International HUL:** the word *International* and proximity to Canada do not establish CBP permission for any particular cross-border flight or an approved commercial carrier. Requires actual owner, CBP and winter authorization.
7. **Harnett Jetport HRJ and Hampton HPT:** daylight/weekdays FBO support and fueling must not be equated with automatic nighttime or weekend turbine ground services; both remain held.
8. **Half Moon Bay HAF and Ingalls Field HSP:** coastal fog and terrain/mountain weather demand specific approaches, minima and aircraft climb, rather than relying on nominal paved length alone.
9. **Conway–Horry County HYW:** concrete 4,401ft runway reports **12,500lb single-wheel** bearing; always compare with actual aircraft gear load and declared directional landing distance.
10. Apply Airlinerra's **exactly six established airport categories**, never four; **no category is assigned now**. Future airport-game eligibility requires operational asphalt or concrete runway **at least 1,200 metres usable direction-specific LDA**, sufficient runway width/load and performance, current dated NOTAM and fuel/handling, airport owner permission and actual certificated charter, cargo or passenger carrier.
