## Cross-airport safeguards

- **South Alabama Regional (79J)** and **Three Forks (9S5)**: multi-user GA, military, gliding, parachute and ultralight traffic are coordination requirements; they do not automatically establish permanent paved runway closure. At 9S5 airport remarks mandate **500 feet of climb before any departure turn**, and 100LL fuel is listed. A current aircraft-specific safety separation plan is still required.
- **John F. Kennedy Memorial (ASX)**: aircraft above **4,000lb must park on CONCRETE**, a binding parking rule—not a maximum approved aircraft takeoff weight or automatic runway closure. Jet A is available by prior permission/call; concrete stand availability and wildlife conditions need confirmation. Wisconsin DOT independently states the parking constraint.
- **Huron County Memorial (BAX)**: turboprop/jet landing fees, waived with purchase of fuel, are a fee/administrative policy rather than a blanket aircraft operations ban; runway load and deer/large-bird hazards remain open.
- **Horseshoe Bend (6M2)**: **4,000lb single-wheel runway limit**, **994ft displaced RW13 threshold**, and rising terrain are real hard constraints. Do not infer acceptable runway landing distance from the 4,502ft physical length.
- **Brush Municipal (7V5)** and **Mount Ida (7M3)**: 6,000lb/12,500lb runway wheel limits and night/approach displaced thresholds require direct declared LDA and aircraft loading checks.
- **Stevenson (7A6), Big Timber (6S0), Terrell County (6R6) and McGehee (7M1)**: physical pavement degradation, differing inspection vintages, shoulder/turnaround drops and faulty airport lighting warrant real repairs or valid inspections—not a procedural waiver.
- **Ravalli County (6S5)**: county rule weight restrictions and special owner permissions are binding for intended aircraft; landing fees must not be confused with owner exceptions.
- **Airlinerra eligibility:** exactly **six established categories** (none assigned here). Only operational asphalt/concrete runway at least **1,200m**, with actual direction-specific landing distance, pavement/load/width, active FAA NOTAM, runway/weather/obstacle performance, legitimate fuel and ground handling and owner and certificated operator approval may become game-eligible.
