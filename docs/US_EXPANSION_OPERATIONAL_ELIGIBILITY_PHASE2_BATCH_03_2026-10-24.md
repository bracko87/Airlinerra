[Airport FAA-derived source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/KGUR)

**Noise and timing restrictions:**
- **5C1 Boerne Stage (TX):** privately owned and public-use, but landing fees/noise-abatement rules; no touch-and-go, stop-and-go or low approaches; arriving turbine aircraft must meet Stage 3 or better. [October 2026 FAA-cycle source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/5C1)
- **L70 Agua Dulce (CA):** runway 4205×50ft with displaced thresholds; closed sunset to sunrise with locally prohibited night operations; no touch-and-go, aerobatics or formation flights. Daytime-only use is not itself paid-charter permission. [October 2026 FAA-cycle source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/L70)

### Interpretation of Phase 2 statuses

- `CONSTRAINT_CONFIRMED_HOLD` = a specific source-reported operating constraint (such as narrow pavement, a published limitation, shared use or a known traffic conflict). It does **not** imply permanent exclusion.
- `SOURCE_IDENTITY_RECONCILIATION` = alias/source identifier history needs resolution; no airport is removed or renamed.
- `EVIDENCE_GAP_HOLD` = public-use/GA facilities may be real, but airline/charter/cargo permission and/or other material evidence is still missing. This status deliberately avoids claiming a legal prohibition.
- `CURRENT_SCREEN_INELIGIBLE` = provisional source screen fails the game runway criterion; no such new determinations were made in this batch.
