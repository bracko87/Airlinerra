## Method and distinction

Phase 1 has completed source research on **1,929/1,929** U.S. expansion candidates. This Phase 2 determines *whether additional evidence is needed before any aircraft/gameplay eligibility decision*. It does **not** certify commercial airport operations, confer owner access rights, verify all current NOTAMs, or authorize game admissions. Historical first-pass notes from the 2026 FAA publication cycle are the primary evidence; particularly serious findings (33U, 6M2, 2W6 and 71J) also had current public records checked in this batch. Every airport remains **`NOT_APPROVED`**.

## Status interpretation

- `CONSTRAINT_CONFIRMED_HOLD` means specific source-reported physical/operational limitations warrant further review; **does not equal permanent rejection** or airport closure unless expressly documented.
- `EVIDENCE_GAP_HOLD` means source listings or GA amenities **do not establish certificated passenger, charter or cargo operator rights**; no restriction is invented.
- `SOURCE_IDENTITY_RECONCILIATION` means conflicting FAA/GPS/legacy codes or names. No airport key is renamed or merged.
- `CURRENT_SCREEN_INELIGIBLE` is a prior provisional failure of Airlinerra's paved-runway screen. **No new airport received this designation in Batch 04.**
