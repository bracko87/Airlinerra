## High-significance distinctions

**Marco Island Executive MKY (Florida):** Collier County's airport-owning authority explicitly confirms the airport is **open 24 hours a day**, while **staffing is 07:00–19:00 daily** and **after-hours ground services must be prearranged** (fees apply). This is **not a mandatory overnight runway closure**. Narrow advancement addresses this issue alone; displaced thresholds, directional LDA, noise/wildlife restrictions, confirmed ground support, owner minimum standards and a licensed operator remain mandatory. Source: https://www.collier.gov/County-Development/Transportation-Management/Airport-Authority/Marco-Island-Executive-Airport

**Spanish Fork/Woodhouse Field SPK (Utah):** The City lists general/business/recreational/emergency aviation, substantial training activity and full-service Jet A. The published preferred VFR arrival/departure procedures are explicitly **recommended and not FAA-enforceable**. Traffic/mountain/Provo-airspace complexity warrants coordination, not a permanent physical runway closure. This narrow distinction advances, without waiving airport permission, flight-date NOTAM, runway load/LDA or carrier certificate. Sources: https://www.spanishfork.gov/business-development/airport/ and https://www.spanishfork.gov/business-development/airport/pilot-information/

**Portland Municipal PLD (Indiana):** FAA chart supplement lists **09/27 5,505×75ft** with **S-12.5** single-wheel weight bearing, while an older City web page still reports 4,002ft and discusses runway extension. Latest FAA geometry takes precedence for triage, but requires date-current as-built/declared landing distances and aircraft wheel-load confirmation. **This genuine capacity limit is NOT a landing fee**, and airport stays held. Sources: https://www.airnav.com/airport/KPLD and https://thecityofportland.net/municipal-airport

**Phillipsburg Municipal PHG (Kansas):** FAA-derived 5,101ft concrete length differs from an older 4,500ft municipal figure. Both gross values clear the basic 1,200m screening length, but actual usable LDA, pavement strength and permit/airframe fit still require resolution before any admission.

**Mountain View MNF (Missouri):** RW10 has a roughly 319ft displaced threshold, with faded pavement markings, 100LL-only listing and no repairs. **The physical 5,005ft pavement cannot be substituted for actual direction-specific LDA**; held.

**Other aircraft and service checks:** Utah and Idaho high-elevation airports **MLD, MLF, U02, U08, U13, U34, U52, U55, U69, U70 and U96** require aircraft hot/high and winter performance, actual runway width/gear loading, fuel and repair contingencies and written owner commercial authorization. **Petit Jean MPJ** is a state-park GA airfield and not nearby Morrilton BDQ. **Philip PHP** has only about 1,219m gross pavement, leaving little LDA margin. **Green River U34** has only historical charter-tour evidence, not a confirmed live certified provider. Current fueling and off-hours services remain open for **MOX** and other seasonal fields.
