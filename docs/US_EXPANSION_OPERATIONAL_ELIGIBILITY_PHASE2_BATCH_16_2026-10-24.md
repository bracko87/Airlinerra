# Airlinerra — U.S. Expansion Operational Eligibility Phase 2, Batch 16 (2026-10-24)

## Scope and correctness

Airlinerra has **1,929** U.S. expansion candidates, already reviewed in separate Phase 1 FAA/airport evidence research. Phase 2 is source-backed operational eligibility triage, **not** final route certification, airport-owner permission, lawful aircraft/operator licensing or automatic gameplay admission. The source-of-truth records are `airlinerra.airport_operational_triage` joined to immutable airport keys in `airlinerra.airport_commercial_verification`. The original 3,140 playable airports are never modified by this batch.

Each new record is saved with a distinct issue category, specific finding, actionable next verification, evidence URL(s), source effective date and UTC review timestamp. The findings are based on previously researched public FAA/airport sources plus independently checked up-to-date public references for especially consequential cases. Temporary closures and outages must always be rechecked before inferring current operational restrictions. Airlinerra's **six** game airport categories remain exactly Local, Regional, Medium, Large, Major Hub, Mega Hub.

## High-priority evidence requiring runway-specific interpretation

### CDS — Childress Municipal, Texas

[Current FAA-derived runway source](https://www.airnav.com/airport/CDS) and [current NOTAM](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KCDS) identify RW04/22 (**4,425×60ft**) as **poor, unmaintained and closed indefinitely**, with closure NOTAM from November 13, 2025 through November 13, 2027. This applies **only to RW04/22**. The separate paved **RW18/36 is 5,949×75ft, GOOD**, but its nonstandard threshold lighting still warrants review. Do not count the closed runway or label the entire airport closed; check currently effective notices, primary runway strength and operator rights.

### CMR — H. A. Clark Memorial Field/Cottonwood, Arizona

[City of Cottonwood's aviation directory](https://cottonwoodaz.gov/770/Airport-Directory) says the city provides **24-hour 100LL self-serve** but **Jet A is currently unavailable**. The directory also describes actual air tour/maintenance providers. No named certified scheduled passenger operation is independently established. Fuel-dependent fleet planning must confirm renewed Jet A availability and lawful charter operation; this is **not** a permanent airport shutdown.

### BTY — Beatty, Nevada

[October 2026 aviation services listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/KBTY) gives Beatty a published 100LL offering, but the provider fuel note says **fuel currently unavailable** (fuel quote dated October 7). These observations differ in purpose; do not assume reliable fuel. Contact operator and check current NOTAM before any aircraft requiring airport refueling. No blanket runway closure or permanent exclusion is inferred.

### ARR / ASX / ASD — Different limits need different treatment

These airports were completed in **Batch 15, not this batch** and are not repeated or included in Batch 16 totals. They remain important cross-check patterns: an individual runway closure (ARR) or aircraft parking requirement (ASX) must not be misclassified as an airport-wide closure or a physical runway weight limit; a displaced threshold (ASD) affects one landing direction rather than published pavement length.
