## Critical operational distinctions

**Henderson Executive (HND):** Official airport owner confirms the airport is open **24h**, while Jet A/FBO services have different staffed windows. FAA runway **17R/35L is 6,501x100ft**, while **17L/35R is 5,001x75ft** with a separately published `OPS DLY 1900-0700` remark requiring interpretation before scheduling. Special-event prior-permission windows **October 16–23** (already elapsed on Oct 25), **November 17–23** and **December 2–13** (upcoming) are temporary date-specific access checks, not indefinite closure. [Airport owner](https://www.hnd.aero/) · [FAA 2026 runway restrictions](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HND).

**Hayward Executive (HWD):** Main **10R/28L 5,694x150ft** runway's displaced thresholds **826ft RW10R** and **676ft RW28L** leave nominal landing pavement of 4,868ft and 5,018ft respectively, not actual published LDA. Separate **10L/28R 3,108ft** is too short for Airlinerra and FAA explicitly closes it when the tower closes; the restriction does not automatically close the main. Connecting taxiways restrict effective usable aircraft loading. **Taxiway Z1 over 94ft wingspan and A1 over 100ft wingspan prohibited**. Binding noise and lighting rules still require current owner/NOTAM checks. [FAA runway and taxiway remarks](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HWD).

**Roscommon County/Blodgett (HTL):** Primary paved **09/27 4,000x75ft** is different from turf **18/36 2,200ft**, closed when snow-covered except ski-equipped aircraft. The secondary snow restriction does not automatically close paved 09/27. Current FAA data list **100LL only**, whereas old research mentioned Jet A: do not infer turbine-fuel readiness. With only ~19m gross length above minimum, actual LDA may still disqualify one or both primary landing directions. [FAA 2026 service and runway record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HTL).

**Hammond Northshore (HDC):** FAA **13/31 asphalt/concrete 6,502x100ft** has **690ft displaced threshold on RW31**, leaving 5,812ft of nominal landing pavement; separately **18/36 concrete 5,001x150ft** has ILS capability and *different* single/double-wheel loading. RW31 REIL fault and approach trees require fresh NOTAM; tower/FBO operations are not blanket airline permission. [FAA runway details](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HDC).

**San Marcos Regional (HYI):** Three operating asphalt runways **08/26 6,330ft**, **13/31 5,601ft** and **17/35 5,214ft** must be treated separately. FAA states **transient airplane parking is at the FBO ramp only**, absent prior management permission elsewhere. Tower 07:00–21:00, wildlife and flight-training operations need scheduling/aircraft-specific checks. [FAA aircraft/ramp operations](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HYI).

**Gwinner (GWR), Three Rivers (HAI) and Sawyer County (HYR):** Short or winter-closed turf secondary runways never count toward the paved minimum and do not themselves disqualify a separate paved primary. HAI's main **4,000ft** is barely above 1,200m. GWR's main 5,000ft good concrete has low **S-14,000lb/D-19,000lb** airplane loading and no repair support. HYR's main **5,003ft excellent asphalt** has stronger published capacity but field conditions are unmonitored overnight and repairs unavailable. [GWR](https://www.airnav.com/airport/GWR) · [HAI](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HAI) · [HYR](https://www.airnav.com/airport/HYR).

**Clinton Regional (GLY):** Excellent concrete **18/36 5,000ft**, FAA S-60,000lb, differs from **04/22 4,001ft fair asphalt** and much lower weight bearing. Neither runway's official directional LDA is established in this screening. [FAA October runway record](https://www.airnav.com/airport/GLY).

**Hawkins Field (HKS):** Operating grooved asphalt **16/34 5,387ft**, FAA S-30,000lb/D-40,000lb/DT-80,000lb, with ILS; short concrete 11/29 **3,431ft** fails the game's runway minimum and its lights are unavailable when tower closes.
