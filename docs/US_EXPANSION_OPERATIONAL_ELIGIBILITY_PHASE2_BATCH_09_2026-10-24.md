## What this phase does

The prior first-pass research is complete for 1,929/1,929 US expansion candidates, but these airports have **not** been certified, approved, classified or activated in the Airlinerra game. Phase 2 separately identifies evidence gaps, runway/weight/weather restrictions, operating closures, and records exactly what must be independently checked before an admission decision. FAA/airport directory content and source notes are evidence leads; they do not substitute for current NOTAMs, airport-owner permission, Part 121/135 or aircraft-specific analysis. Even a published temporary outage must be date-scoped.

All new records are linked to original immutable `airport_key` values in private-schema `airlinerra.airport_operational_triage`. Each includes specific airport source evidence, current triage issue, action required, date and a UTC review timestamp. No original game-facing tables were mutated.

**U14 Nephi Municipal (UT):** [Contemporary NOTAM listing](https://www.iflightplanner.com/Airports/U14) shows **6,300ft physical runway**, but October 21–30 declared landing distance is **2,550ft both directions**. Temporary TORA/TODA/ASDA also significantly shorter. This is under Airlinerra's **1,200m usable-runway interpretation** and requires a new review at the October 30 expiry. The original physical paved runway is longer than 1,200m, so this does **not** prove permanent physical-runway ineligibility. Airport REILs and precision approach minima also affected.

**FPR Treasure Coast International (FL):** [Airport authority](https://flytci.com/about/about-fpr/) confirms FAA **Part 139 airport certification on October 2, 2024**, allowing it to pursue qualified commercial airline operations; as of this triage no operating scheduled route was independently confirmed. **U62 Mackay (ID):** [FAA-derived airport report](https://www.airnav.com/airport/U62) describes **very rough chipseal**, uneven surface, significantly deficient runway paint and approach powerline, with no winter maintenance. This is an aircraft safety/condition **hold**, not a claim of official closure.

**SVE Susanville (CA)**: Source records a 12,000lb aircraft owner constraint. **T69 Sinton (TX)**: 8,000lb single-wheel on the narrow paved runway, secondary turf closed. **SOA Sonora (TX)** and **T50 Menard (TX)** have substantial threshold displacement, where reported nominal physical length overstates direction-specific usable LDA. **SKI, SQI and Y70** have short or turf secondary strips; those do not necessarily disqualify their paved main runway.
