## Key requirements and distinctions

**Mount Vernon Outland (MVN):** A reported inactive FAA Part 139 certificate is a restriction on specific covered air-carrier passenger operations; it is not evidence of an airport-wide physical runway shutdown. The FAA/owner certificate mismatch must be verified before any covered passenger operation. [FAA Part 139](https://www.faa.gov/airports/airport_safety/part139_cert).

**Montgomery-Gibbs Executive (MYF):** San Diego lists primary 10L/28R 4598x150ft, but a 1176ft displaced RW28R threshold, versus 1199ft in another FAA-based listing. The resulting approximate RW28R landing distance is below 1200m; two alternate strips of ~3400ft are also below the game's minimum. RW10L requires independently confirmed declared distances and local takeoff restrictions, not automatic approval. FAA notes wrong-surface/parallel runway risks. [San Diego airport](https://www.sandiego.gov/airports/montgomery) · [FAA airport cautions](https://www.faa.gov/flight_deck/myf).

**Winchester Regional (OKV):** A separate turbine/jet traffic-pattern altitude does not itself close the paved runway. Aircraft compatibility, current NOTAM and certified commercial operator remain pending.

**Ontario Municipal (ONO):** Seasonal glider and firefighting traffic requires coordination but is not a permanent physical closure. Runway load and airport-operator permission are still pending.

**Monte Vista (MVI):** 7611ft high-elevation airport with only 60ft paved width and ~12500lb single-wheel load remains held for safe aircraft-specific performance.

**Lone Pine (O26):** Physical pavement near the 1200m minimum must not be confused with the shorter dirt strip, and Jet A is only arranged ahead of time.

**Part 139 and aircraft eligibility:** The game's minimum operational **1200m asphalt/concrete runway** must be verified as **usable landing distance by direction**, alongside wheel load, width, obstacle, weather/terrain and pavement safety, current NOTAM, fuel/ground services, written owner permission and certificated operator. A next-gate advance does not waive any remaining requirement. Exactly **six** established airport categories are valid; no category has been assigned here.
