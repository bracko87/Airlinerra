## Five clarified restriction scopes

- **HCR Heber Valley, Utah:** [City fee policy](https://www.heberut.gov/404/Aircraft-Landing-Fees) describes billable landing fees, not a runway closure. Municipal minimum operating standards still apply, and aircraft high-altitude suitability must be assessed.
- **RIL Rifle/Garfield County, Colorado:** [Airport owner landing fees](https://www.rifleairport.com/landing-fees/) are an economic threshold, not a maximum permitted aircraft weight. Actual pavement/owner/terrain restrictions remain.
- **PNE Northeast Philadelphia:** [Airport operator](https://www.phl.org/PNE/pilot-information) lists distinct 7,000ft paved primary and 5,000ft paved secondary runways. The FAA publishes different direction-specific LDA and runway widths. Future routes need the appropriate runway, aircraft type and airport approval.
- **HUT Hutchinson, Kansas:** [FAA March 2026 Chart Supplement](https://aeronav.faa.gov/Upload_313-d/supplements/CS_NC_20260319.pdf) verifies 7,003ft paved 13/31 primary with LDA 7,003ft each way, plus two other distinct paved runways with their own ratings.
- **TPL Temple/Draughon–Miller, Texas:** [FAA-derived airport data](https://vfrmap.com/fe?q=KTPL&req=get_afd) separately show 7,000ft 16/34 and 4,740ft 03/21 paved runways and operational differences.

These five have progressed **only because a particular constraint was clarified**. This is not an airport-wide or aircraft-independent safety sign-off and does not assign a game category.

## Strong restriction still held

**MKC Charles B Wheeler Downtown** [airport authority](https://flymkc.com/services) states **air-carrier operations with more than 30 passenger seats require prior airport-authority permission**. This is not a closure or a universal ban on all commercial operators, but the seat-count/permission restriction is real and must be enforceable for any future eligible aircraft.

Remaining 24 holds include mountain density-altitude performance (AEJ, AFO, ANK, AXX, BCE, BPI, C08, DIJ, EKS, EMM, SAA, 33V), aircraft wheel-load and geometry limitations (TYL, X60, SUS), fuel availability (38U), tribal jurisdiction (E91), military/USFS operations (MTN, SCH, RMG, SIY), special test/spaceport and maintenance fields (MHV, MZJ), and coastal weather/traffic at NEW. Prior/current scheduled airline service is **not** a prerequisite for the airline-management simulation.
