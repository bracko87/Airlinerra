## Selected high-priority evidence

### AKR — Akron Fulton, Ohio: positive licensed air-charter lead

[American Winds' advertised Part 135 charter service](https://www.americanwinds.edu/private-charter/) gives **Air Carrier Certificate AYKA724K** for private passenger and time-critical cargo, supported by an [independent operator-directory listing](https://eflight.com/operators/american-winds-inc). Actual current certificate scope, available aircraft and airport landing authorization still must be checked. Do not misclassify legitimate advertised private charters as no commercial aviation, and do not infer a scheduled airline.

### BWG — Bowling Green Warren County, Kentucky: airport commercial agreement required

[City ordinance §3-2.01](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/bowlinggreen/latest/bowlinggreen_ky/0-0-0-55202) requires an **Airport Board written agreement or license** for commercial activities, alongside insurance and airport rules. The presence of charter marketing or a large paved runway does not waive this restriction.

### GON — Groton–New London, Connecticut: Action Airlines is listed

The [Connecticut Airport Authority's directory](https://ctairports.org/airports/groton-newlondon/services/) expressly names Action Airlines as a private charter tenant. Validate operating certificate and fleet while preserving this positive on-airport charter evidence.

### LOZ — London–Corbin, Kentucky: owner states no current charter service

[London–Corbin Airport's owner website](https://www.london-corbinairport.com/about-us.html) says charter service is **not currently offered**. The airport continues hosting corporate, private, military and cargo aircraft, and historic Piedmont passenger airline service does not establish modern schedules. The review asks whether an independently licensed outside charter/cargo carrier may lawfully visit, not whether the whole runway is closed.

### HQM — Bowerman Airport, Washington: contradictory fuel evidence

[Port of Grays Harbor's Bowerman Airport listing](https://www.portofgraysharbor.com/bowerman-airport) shows a 5,000ft main runway and Jet A, but inconsistent descriptions of 100LL avgas availability. Actual fuel stock must be confirmed directly. Cross-border flights also require current CBP clearance; an "International" label alone does not grant admission.

### AGC, AHN, BKE, CGF, FYV and MMU: positive charter/aviation business evidence

- **AGC:** Allegheny County authority notes regular business charter demand, without a verified named fleet.
- **AHN:** Athens Ben Epps airport authority lists air freight and charter business activity.
- **BKE:** Baker Aircraft advertises charter from Baker City.
- **CGF:** Cuyahoga County names Flexjet and charter/taxi services.
- **FYV:** Fayetteville Drake Field city lists Jett Aircraft charter activity, but no scheduled airline at FYV.
- **MMU:** Morristown Municipal identifies Short Hills Aviation as a charter provider.

Actual operators, certifications and planned flights remain separate checks before gameplay admission.

### Other case-specific operational limits

- **AND Anderson Regional:** source-reported runway cracking and helicopter traffic routing require aircraft/load checks; not a total closure.
- **BDE Baudette and CTB Cut Bank:** airport international names and Canadian-border locations do not provide unrestricted customs access.
- **FTY Fulton County Brown Field:** local corporate/towered GA airport, separate from ATL airline service.
- **LWT Lewistown:** owner says no commercial air service; clarify how that applies to independently permitted charter/cargo.
- **IKK Greater Kankakee:** forklift and freight offload capabilities are **not proof** that an air cargo airline flies there.
- **MIV Millville and MLS Miles City:** multiple paved runways must be assessed individually, with no automatic capacity transfer.
- **OSU Ohio State Don Scott Field and BAK Columbus Municipal:** inactive Part 139 certificate status is not evidence that the airfield itself is physically closed.
- **ELO Ely:** older air-taxi flight percentages are not reliable proof of current air charter routes.
