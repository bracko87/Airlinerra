Airlinerra is an online fictional airline-management game. Managers register with an email address, confirm it, and may create an airline when the selected world is open. Accounts must not be used for impersonation, cheating, or interference with the service.

In-game economy

Each newly activated airline starts with $5,000,000 in virtual game currency and zero aircraft. This balance is not real money, cannot be redeemed for cash, and is awarded only after server-authorized creation.
