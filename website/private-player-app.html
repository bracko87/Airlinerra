HOLDING COMPANY · PREVIEW

Holding companies

See when your airline may qualify for a holding and what businesses you could manage under it.

When can I open a holding?

Your original airline may qualify 60 calendar days after registration. No flights or daily logins are required to complete the waiting period.

Days remaining Checking…
Checking registration age…

The date comes from your airline's registration record and the server clock. Meeting the 60-day requirement does not automatically open holding creation; that feature is not released yet.

How your holding could look

Your existing airline becomes the first subsidiary. You can later open other companies, each with separate finances and any required operating approvals.

Parent holding company Owns and funds the companies below
Original airline Already yours · moves under the holding Existing operator approvals continue within their valid scope
Regional airline New subsidiary · optional Own airline operating licence and AOC required
Cargo airline New subsidiary · optional Own AOC and cargo approvals where required
Maintenance company New subsidiary · optional Maintenance approval, e.g. Part-145 where applicable
Charter / VIP airline New subsidiary · optional Appropriate commercial operator approvals required
Future flight academy — airline infrastructure Initially, your airline could train its own pilots using instructors, simulators and suitable training aircraft. It would be airline infrastructure, not a new holding subsidiary. Formal pilot-licensing courses still need the applicable training-organisation approvals (such as ATO or DTO in Europe). An academy serving outside customers could become a separate business later.

Illustrative structure only — no subsidiaries have been created. Check the Licences & Permits page for requirements and how to obtain approvals.

Holding registration, subsidiary creation and money transfers are not available yet. Viewing this page does not create a company or charge your airline.
