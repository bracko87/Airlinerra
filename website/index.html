GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
takeoff?

Here are the fundamental rules we have already decided for Airlinerra's first release.

Does the game use historical eras?

No. Airlinerra will not offer historical-era scenarios. All worlds follow a real-time calendar.

How fast does time pass in the game?

One real-world day equals one in-game day. This is the basic time rule for all game worlds.

How many worlds will be available?

Our initial architecture is planned for three independent game worlds, but World 01 is the only world planned to open at launch. Worlds 02 and 03 will follow later.

Is Airlinerra a free-to-play game?

No. Airlinerra is being designed as a paid-to-play game with a seven-day introductory trial. Subscription pricing and terms have not been decided yet.

Can I register or subscribe today?

Not yet. This is an early website template, before accounts, game worlds and billing have been launched. The registration process will be introduced later.
