Frequently Asked Questions

What is Airlinerra?

A browser-based multiplayer airline management game where each manager creates an airline, chooses a home airport and plans a network, fleet and flight schedules.

How much money do I start with?

Each newly activated airline receives $5,000,000 in virtual in-game currency and starts with zero aircraft. This has no real-world cash value.

How does the game clock work?

World time follows real calendar time in Germany (Europe/Berlin). One real day equals one in-game day.

Which game worlds are available?

Horizon is World 1 and is being tested. Atlas and Odyssey are reserved for future independent game worlds.

Can I register or create an airline?

Registration and game operations are currently limited while testing continues. Public access depends on the current release status.

Does the seven-day trial charge me?

No payment information is required for the seven-day trial. There is no automatic payment or subscription when it ends.

What does Premium cost?

Subscription prices and billing are not announced yet. See Pricing for the current status.

Where can I get help?

Contact contact@nextqueststudio.net. You can also open Help and Guide inside your airline dashboard.
