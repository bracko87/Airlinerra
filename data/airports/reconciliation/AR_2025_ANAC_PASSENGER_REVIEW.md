# Argentina ANAC 2025 named-airport total passenger evidence

Source: [official ANAC December 2025 report](https://docs.anac.gob.ar/index.php/s/pk8HDnYWX5X3aKL/download/Informe%20Mensual%20Diciembre%202025.pdf), section **Pasajeros Totales por Aeropuerto**, 38 individually named airports plus an unallocated **Otros** aggregate. Airport-reported volumes are in **thousands** and rounded. The source counts embarked/disembarked passengers for regular **and** nonregular commercial flights. The most recent three months may be provisional as at the 31 December 2025 snapshot. Do not treat reported values as independently audited exact annual counts or current 2026 flight rights.

Crosswalk is exact by confirmed ICAO code and original OurAirports airport ID, **not name fuzzy matching**. Units are converted correctly by removing the grouping dot in the source cell (e.g., 17.738 thousand = approximately 17,738,000 people). Missing named-airport rows are **UNKNOWN**, never 'no service'. Only 38 positive named-airport observations are entered; **Otros** is not distributed arbitrarily.

A new technical runway recovery accompanies this batch: SABE airport ID 5771, OurAirports runway ID 233011, officially verified 2,350m concrete AIP runway. The historical Group 46 commercial snapshot remains preserved separately. All category results remain provisional and separate from the master.
