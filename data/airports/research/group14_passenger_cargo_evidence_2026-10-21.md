## Source leads and verified evidence

- **Japan**: MLIT official annual airport management returns include airport-specific landing, passenger, freight and mail totals, including 2024 and 2025. This is the preferred source for mapping individual JP airports. https://www.mlit.go.jp/koku/15_bf_000185.html . The MLIT 2024 airline transportation release is *not* airport-specific and must not be copied onto individual airports: https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/press/joho05_hh_000838.html . Airport-based cargo survey (2024) also at https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/files?cycle=0&cycle_facet=tclass1%3Atclass2&layout=datalist&page=1&tclass1=000001230105&tclass2=000001230108&tclass3val=0&toukei=00600810&tstat=000001078016 .
- **Israel**: Israel Airports Authority publishes individual Ben Gurion annual activity reports: https://www.iaa.gov.il/en/about/aeronautical-information/annualreport . The 2024 report records **14,532,608 total passengers** and **347,707 tons cargo** for Ben Gurion, *not all Israeli airports*. 2024 report: https://www.iaa.gov.il/media/35jf3jzt/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA%D7%99-2024-%D7%90%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%A9.pdf .
- **Jordan**: Ministry of Transport 2024 annual report, aviation statistics, reports **8,969,810 airport passengers and 80,335 tons cargo in 2024 across three Jordanian airports**. Country-wide combined totals must not be assigned to each airport. https://www.mot.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/ar/eb_list_page/annual_report2024___final__21-8-2025.pdf . Airport-level breakdown requires further verification.
- **Kazakhstan**: Official Civil Aviation Committee statistical page reports **29,447,594 airport passengers in 2024** country-wide: https://caa.gov.kz/ru/statistika . A separate Ministry of Transport release reports **31.8m passengers and 173.3k tons cargo in 2025** across Kazakhstan's airports: https://www.gov.kz/org/transport/press/news/details/1169468 . Different publication definitions or rounding mean preserve source-specific dates and figures, do not substitute a country total as an airport total.
- **Iraq**: Airport-by-airport passenger and cargo evidence still requires authoritative verification. Do not assign unsupported numbers to Baghdad, Basra, Najaf, Erbil, Sulaymaniyah or other qualifying airfields.
